| 4. | Clause 1, Article 15: Committees and supporting apparatus of the Board of Directors. | The Board of Directors may establish Committees/Councils under the Board of Directors to assist the Board of Directors in performing its duties and exercising its powers in accordance with this Regulation, the Corporation’s Charter, and applicable laws. | Amendments and supplements: 1. The Board of Directors may establish the Board of Directors Office and other sub-committees to assist the Board of Directors in performing its duties and exercising its powers in accordance with these Regulations, the Corporation’s Charter, and applicable laws. 2. The Board of Directors may establish specialized committees under the Board of Directors to give advisory, consultancy, and supervisory support to the Board of Directors in specialized areas, including audit, human resources, remuneration, strategy, investment, risk management, sustainability, and other relevant fields. 3. The Board of Directors shall decide on the naming, number of personnel, and appointment or dismissal of personnel of the units specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article. 4. The operations of the units specified in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article shall comply with the organizational and operational regulations issued by the Board of Directors. 5. Conclusions and recommendations of the Committees are advisory in nature only and serve as a basis for the Board of Directors to consider and decide within its authority. | Based on the governance needs of the Corporation. |
