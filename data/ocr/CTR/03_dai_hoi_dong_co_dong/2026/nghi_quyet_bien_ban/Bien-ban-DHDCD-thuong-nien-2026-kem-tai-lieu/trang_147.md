The scale of assets and business results of the Parent Company in 2025 continued to grow positively compared to 2024, with a high growth rate of profits, reflecting sustained operational efficiency.

- **Financial structure**

Total assets as of December 31, 2025 reached VND 7,917 billion, up 17.7% compared to the beginning of the year. The asset structure showed a trend of increasing the proportion of current assets (up 26.0%), while long-term assets slightly decreased (down 7.5%), indicating that the Company actively allocated resources to business operations and improved capital flexibility.

Regarding capital resources, total liabilities increased by 19.9%, mainly due to a 24.1% rise in short-term liabilities, in line with the need for additional working capital. Owner’s equity increased by 11.5%, continuing to be strengthened by business performance. Overall, the capital structure remained balanced, meeting operational and development requirements.

- **Business results**

In 2025, total revenue reached VND 11,934.3 billion, an increase of 17.7% compared to 2024, while total expenses rose by 18.2%, corresponding to the expansion of operations. Profit before tax reached VND 726.6 billion, up 10.1%, and profit after tax reached VND 587.4 billion, up 10.5%.

Although the growth rate of expenses was higher than revenue growth, the Company still maintained positive profit growth, indicating that cost management and operational efficiency were appropriately controlled in line with the scale of expansion.
