4. Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives are entitled to log into the system and conduct electronic voting from 08:00 on April 15, 2026 until prior to the announcement of the closure of the Electronic Voting System at the General Meeting.

5. Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives must present ID card/Citizen Identity Card or other valid identification documents, invitation letter and power of attorney (if any) for verification by the Shareholder Eligibility Verification Committee (for in-person attendees); complete registration procedures and receive instructions from the Organizing Committee on accessing the Electronic Voting System. The identification information used for AGM registration must match the shareholder information registered for ownership of CTR shares.

6. Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives must comply with other applicable laws, the Company’s Charter, internal corporate governance regulations and these Regulations. They must comply with the direction of the Presidium and the Organizing Committee, and refrain from causing disruption or disorder at the General Meeting.

Article 5. Rights and Obligations of the Presidium

1. The Chairman of the Board of Directors shall act as the Chairperson of the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS). The Chairperson shall nominate additional members to the Presidium to assist in conducting the GMS, subject to approval by the GMS via the Electronic Voting System prior to the opening of the Meeting.

2. Duties of the Presidium:
- Conduct the Meeting in accordance with the agenda approved by the GMS.
- Manage and facilitate the discussion session among shareholders.
- Respond to matters raised by the Meeting in an appropriate manner.
- Conclude issues discussed at the Meeting and preside over the adoption of the Meeting’s resolutions.
- Propose amendments to the agenda; decide postponement or suspension of the Meeting; change the meeting venue; determine necessary security measures to maintain order, safety, and security of the Meeting; and perform other duties in accordance with the Company’s Charter.
- Members of the Presidium shall perform their duties as assigned and directed by the Chairperson.

Article 6. Rights and Obligations of the Shareholder Eligibility Verification Committee

- Verify the eligibility of Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives to attend the AGM in accordance with applicable laws and the Meeting Regulations.
