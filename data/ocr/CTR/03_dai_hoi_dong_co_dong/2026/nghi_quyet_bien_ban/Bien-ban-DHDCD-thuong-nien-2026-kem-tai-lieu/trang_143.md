- The Executive Board has implemented multiple solutions to enhance the Corporation’s management capacity and operational efficiency, with a strong focus on training and developing the management team and building a pipeline of successor personnel. At the same time, operational processes at business centers and provincial/city construction branches have been standardized, gradually strengthening the role of provincial units in local business activities.
- In 2025, the Executive Board carried out organizational restructuring toward a leaner and more efficient model. The nationwide branch system was reorganized from 63 provincial construction branches (CNCTs) down to 34 CNCTs, with 29 CNCTs being dissolved in line with adjustments to provincial and municipal administrative boundaries. The Corporation also reviewed and standardized job functions and responsibilities, developed competency frameworks, career development pathways, and job grading systems, and implemented a digital performance management system (PMS) to standardize KPI assignment and work performance evaluation processes.
- The Corporation continues to organize its business activities under an integrated ecosystem model: “Consulting – Design – Construction – Equipment Supply – Operation – Optimization,” contributing to enhanced professionalism, resource optimization, and improved operational efficiency.

The SB’s opinions and recommendations have been duly received and directed for review and implementation by relevant units.
- In addition, the Executive Board has continued to focus on and effectively carry out external relations, shareholder and investor relations, as well as engagement with state regulatory authorities, thereby contributing to enhancing the Corporation’s reputation, brand image, and market position.
