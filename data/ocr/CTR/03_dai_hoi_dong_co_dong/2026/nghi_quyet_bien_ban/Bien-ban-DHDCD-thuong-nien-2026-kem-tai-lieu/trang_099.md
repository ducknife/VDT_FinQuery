# REGULATIONS ON ORGANIZATION AND VOTING
# AT THE 2026 ANNUAL GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS
# OF VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION JOINT STOCK CORPORATION

## CHAPTER I. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### Article 1. Scope and Applicable Subjects

1. These Regulations applies to the organization and conduct of the 2026 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (hereinafter referred to as the “AGM”) of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation (hereinafter referred to as the “Company”), held in the form of a hybrid meeting combining in-person and online attendance, and regulates voting by electronic voting method.

2. These Regulations specify the rights and obligations of shareholders, shareholder representatives, meeting participants and the bodies responsible for organizing and supporting the AGM; as well as the conditions and procedures for conducting the meeting and voting on matters within the authority of the AGM.

### Article 2. Working Principles of the General Meeting

1. Transparency, fairness and democracy.

2. Ensuring the rights and interests of shareholders and the Company.

3. Compliance with applicable laws, the Company’s Charter, the internal regulations on corporate governance and these Regulations.

### Article 3. Definitions and Interpretation

1. “Organizing Committee” means the body established to support the preparation and organization of the AGM.

2. “General Meeting” means the 2026 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation.

3. “Shareholder” means a person who owns CTR shares and whose name appears on the list of shareholders entitled to attend the AGM as of the final registration date (March 9, 2026) as provided by the Vietnam Securities Depository and Clearing Corporation (“VSDC”).

4. “Shareholder’s Representative” means the legal representative of an institutional shareholder or a person duly authorized in writing by a shareholder to attend the AGM and exercise electronic voting rights.
