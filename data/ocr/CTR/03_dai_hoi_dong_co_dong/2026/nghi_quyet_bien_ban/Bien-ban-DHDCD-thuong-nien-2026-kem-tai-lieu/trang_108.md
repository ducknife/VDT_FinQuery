# REPORT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
## On the 2025 performance results and 2026 orientation.

To: General Meeting of Shareholders of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation.

The Board of Directors (BOD) respectfully report the General Meeting of Shareholders of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation (hereinafter referred to as the “General Meeting”) the 2025 operation results and the 2026 orientation as follows:

## I. THE OPERATIONAL BOARD OF DIRECTORS IN 2025

### 1. General information

- The global political environment remained complicated with ongoing conflicts in various regions and U.S. tariff policies creating barriers to global trade. Domestically, GDP recorded strong growth of over 8% and the macroeconomic environment remained stable. The Party and Government issued numerous resolutions and legal documents to promote economic development.
- The year 2025 was marked by unpredictable natural disasters and extreme weather conditions, placing significant pressure on the Corporation’s network infrastructure and business operations. In addition, 2025 was also a year of major national events such as A50, A80, and Party Congresses at various levels. As the Corporation is a pioneering unit in deploying technical infrastructure to ensure communications for these events, this represented both a major challenge and a source of pride for its employees.
- Despite these difficulties, the Corporation successfully completed the business and production plan assigned by the General Meeting of Shareholders and maintained double-digit growth.
- In terms of corporate governance, the Corporation promptly restructured its provincial branches following administrative mergers at the provincial/city level, and implemented strategic business orientations as well as workforce development strategies for the 2026–2030 period, aiming for high growth and sustainable development.

##### 2.1.1. Business results 2025

- The consolidated revenue in 2025 reached VND 14,058 billion, equivalent to 101% of the plan and an increase of 11% compared to the same period in 2024.
