5. “Online Meeting” means a form of organizing the AGM using electronic means and modern information technology solutions to transmit images and audio via the Internet, enabling Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives at different locations to follow the meeting, participate in discussions, and vote on matters within the authority of the AGM.

6. “Electronic Voting” means the exercise of voting rights by Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives using computers, mobile phones, or other Internet-connected devices through the Electronic Voting System as prescribed and notified by the Company.

7. “Electronic Voting System” means the system that provides Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives with tools to exercise their relevant rights, including but not limited to voting rights when attending the AGM.

8. “Identification Information” means the information required to accurately identify an entity in a given context.

CHAPTER II.  
RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF SHAREHOLDERS, MEETING PARTICIPANTS AND SUPPORTING THE BODIES

Article 4. Rights and Obligations of Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives

1. Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives attending the General Meeting are entitled to vote on all matters within the authority of the Meeting in accordance with applicable laws and the Company’s Charter by the Electronic Voting method. Each share shall carry one vote on the Electronic Voting System.

2. Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives attending the General Meeting are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of access credentials (authentication codes, OTPs, and other identification factors) to ensure that only the Shareholder/Shareholder Representative can access the Online AGM System to attend, discuss, express opinions, and perform electronic voting. The voting results of the Shareholder/Shareholder Representative on the Electronic Voting System shall be deemed final. Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives shall take the sole responsibility before the law and the Company for the electronic voting results recorded on the Electronic Voting System.

3. In the event that login credentials and/or other identification factors are lost, stolen, disclosed or suspected to be compromised, the Shareholder/Shareholder Representative must promptly notify the Company so that appropriate measures can be taken to ensure security. The Shareholder/Shareholder Representative shall take the sole responsibility for any damages, losses, and risks arising due to their fault.
