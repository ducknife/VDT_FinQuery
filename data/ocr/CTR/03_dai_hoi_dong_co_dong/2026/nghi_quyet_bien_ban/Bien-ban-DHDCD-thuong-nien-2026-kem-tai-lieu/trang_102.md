- Respond to inquiries or identify issues requiring review regarding shareholder eligibility.
- The Head of the Shareholder Eligibility Verification Committee shall report the verification results to the AGM and confirm that the conditions for convening the AGM are satisfied under Article 10 of these Regulations.
- Perform other duties as assigned by the Presidium.

**Article 7. Rights and Obligations of the Vote Counting Committee**

The Vote Counting Committee is a specialized body of the General Meeting, comprising a Head of the Committee and other members as approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders. The Vote Counting Committee consists of:
- The Vote Counting Team;
- The Vote Supervision Team, including: An Independent Member of the Board of Directors (BOD) and the other shareholder.

The Vote Counting Committee shall have the following rights and obligations:
- Compile and report the voting results to the AGM.
- Receive and handle issues arising in connection with the exercise of voting rights on the online voting system.
- Be responsible for the truthfulness and accuracy of the vote counting results.

**Article 8. Rights and Obligations of the Secretariat**

The Secretariat is a specialized body of the General Meeting, whose composition and number of members are nominated by the Chairperson and approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders. Its duties are:
- Recording fully and accurately all contents and proceedings of the Meeting.
- Summarizing opinions and discussions at the Meeting.
- Drafting the minutes and resolutions of the Meeting.
- Receiving registrations to speak, shareholders’ speeches, and related correspondence and documents for the Meeting.
- Collecting, safeguarding and submitting all documents, materials and shareholders’ feedback forms at the Meeting to the Board of Directors.
- Performing other supporting tasks as assigned by the Presidium.
