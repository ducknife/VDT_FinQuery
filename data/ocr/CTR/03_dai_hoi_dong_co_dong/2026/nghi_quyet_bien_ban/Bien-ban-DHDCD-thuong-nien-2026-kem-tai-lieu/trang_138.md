## I. OPERATION RESULTS OF THE SUPERVISORY BOARD IN 2025

### 1. Organization and operations of the Supervisory Board

In 2025, the Supervisory Board (SB) of the Corporation underwent changes in its personnel structure in accordance with Resolution No. 17/NQ-VCC dated April 16, 2025.

After consolidation, the SB operates with three full-time members, including one Head of the Supervisory Board and two members, ensuring compliance with the structure prescribed by law and the Corporation’s Charter.

Based on its assigned functions and duties, the SB has allocated responsibilities to each member according to their respective areas of expertise, and has implemented supervisory activities in accordance with the plan approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS). The organization and operations of the SB are conducted on the principles of independence, objectivity, and compliance with applicable laws, the Corporation’s Charter, and the organizational and operational Regulations of the SB.

### 2. Summary of the Supervisory Board’s activities in 2025

- In 2025, the Supervisory Board (SB) held five meetings to evaluate the Corporation’s operational performance in each period and implement key supervisory tasks. The meeting agenda focused on assessing the 2024 operation results and quarterly performance in 2025, analyzing business operations and financial conditions, reviewing financial statements, and identifying potential risks in the management and administration of the Corporation’s activities.
