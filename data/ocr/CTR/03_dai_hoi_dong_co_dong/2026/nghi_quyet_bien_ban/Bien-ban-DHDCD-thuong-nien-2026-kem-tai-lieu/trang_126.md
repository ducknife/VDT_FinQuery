## 5.2. Infrastructure for Lease Investment:

- Promoting the search and development of renewable energy investment opportunities: Strengthen project sourcing and engagement from the branch/company level, focusing on large-scale customers with high demand for solar energy such as factories, industrial parks, export processing zones, government agencies, schools, hospitals with energy-saving budgets, and agricultural farms. In parallel, assess project feasibility and actively promote the development of wind power investment projects.

- Expanding collaboration channels and consultancy partners for shared BTS infrastructure locations: Assign Work Orders (WOs) to provincial units to engage and work with telecom operators to evaluate progress and implementation outcomes at provincial level. Designate Branch/Center Directors as focal points to directly engage and maintain regular follow-ups with VNPT and MobiFone in their respective areas of responsibility.

## 5.3. B2B Construction:

### Sales Channels

- Strengthen engagement with local authorities and expand the search for public investment projects at the Branch/Center level (CNCT).

- Develop a strategic customer base in the real estate sector:

  - Form consortiums with reputable construction and M&E contractors to enhance capabilities and meet requirements for large-scale bidding packages.

  - Increase engagement and sign strategic cooperation agreements with existing VCC clients to expand the proportion of “repeated sale”

  - Build sales and project acquisition networks for FDI-related opportunities at key CNCTs through investment consulting firms, provincial/city Departments of Planning and Investment, and Management Boards of industrial zones and export processing zones.

  - Explore opportunities to execute construction projects in international markets, particularly in countries where the Group has invested or maintains representative offices.

- Explore opportunities to implement construction projects in international markets, particularly in countries where the Group has invested or has representative offices.

### Execution and control channels:

- Build a key workforce at the Center with sufficient capability to execute key national projects and FDI projects:

  - Strengthen recruitment for key positions through recruitment platforms and construction-related professional groups on Facebook.

  - Contact recruitment networks within major construction companies in the market (e.g., Coteccons, Hoa Binh, Central, etc.) to identify potential candidates.
