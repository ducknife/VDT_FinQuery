# PROPOSAL TO THE GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS
## Re: The selection of the auditor for the 2026 Financial Statements

To: The General Meeting of Shareholders of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation.

Pursuant to the Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14 dated June 17, 2020;  
Pursuant to the Law on Securities No. 54/2019/QH14 dated November 26, 2019;  
Pursuant to the Charter of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation;  
In consideration of the rights and obligations of the Supervisory Board in accordance with applicable laws and the Corporation’s Charter;

The Supervisory Board respectfully submits this Proposal to the General Meeting of Shareholders for consideration the selection of the auditor for the Corporation’s 2026 Financial Statements as follows:

## 1. Requirements for the auditing firm
- Being an independent auditing firm approved by the State Securities Commission of Vietnam to audit public interest entities in the securities sector for 2026.
- Having reputable standing, professional competence and auditing experience appropriate to the scale and business lines of the Corporation.

## 2. List of proposed auditing firms
Based on the review and assessment of the capacity and reputation of independent auditing firms, the Supervisory Board proposes the following list of auditing firms for consideration by the General Meeting of Shareholders:

1. Ernst & Young Vietnam Co., Ltd  
2. Deloitte Vietnam Company Limited  
3. AASC Auditing Firm Company Limited  
4. UHY Auditing and Consulting Company Limited  

## 3. Recommendation
The Supervisory Board respectfully recommends that the General Meeting of Shareholders:

- To approve the above list of auditing firms to conduct the audit of the Corporation’s 2026 Financial Statements.
- To authorize the Board of Directors to select one auditing firm from the above list to perform the review of the semi-annual financial statements and the audit of the 2026 Financial Statements of the Corporation; based on such selection, the General Director shall sign and implement the contract in accordance with regulations.

We hereby respectfully send this Proposal to the General Meeting of Shareholders for consideration and approval.

ON BEHALF OF SUPERVISORY BOARD
