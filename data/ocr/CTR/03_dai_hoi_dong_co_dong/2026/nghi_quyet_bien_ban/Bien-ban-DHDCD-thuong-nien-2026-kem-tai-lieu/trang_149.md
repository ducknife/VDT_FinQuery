2. Some Opinions and Recommendations
To enhance the effectiveness of management and operations, the Supervisory Board makes the following recommendations to the Board of Directors (BOD) and the Executive Board:
- The Company should continue to strengthen the review and inspection of legal documentation related to domestic and market business activities, ensuring that all production and business operations strictly comply with and uphold the law.
- In the context of ongoing global and regional economic fluctuations that may affect business operations, the Company should proactively develop appropriate management and operational scenarios to promptly adapt to market changes, ensuring sustained growth and operational stability.
