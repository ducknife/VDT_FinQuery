5. Specific Tasks by Business fields:
5.1. Operation & Exploitation (O&E):
✦ Enhancing the quality of O&E technical workforce
- Workforce planning and ensuring adequate staffing for O&E technical personnel:
- Calculate manpower norms down to the Technical Center (TC) to ensure workforce redundancy and improve labor productivity.
- Ensure staffing alignment with defined norms: Apply RPA to automatically send daily alerts to O&E management levels regarding personnel surplus or shortage at the TC level, enabling timely corrective actions.
- Strengthening technical training programs for O&E personnel: Implement training programs through onboarding courses for new field technicians (FTs), as well as targeted training courses for underperforming FTs.
✦ Applying digital transformation in O&M activities
- Apply AI to analyze field images to monitor progress and quality of O&E activities.
- Apply RPA to support automated alerts on workforce surplus/shortage in accordance with staffing norms.
✦ Strengthening network resilience
- Proactively reinforce critical transmission network points (average 5,000 points per quarter).
- Coordinate with VTNet to mitigate storm and weather-related impacts on infrastructure under VTNet’s plan (after each storm, jointly assess damages with VTNet, conduct field surveys, develop solutions, and make action plans).
