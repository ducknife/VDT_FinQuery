1. Shareholders whose names appear on the list of shareholders entitled to attend the General Meeting as of the record date shall have the right to attend in person or authorize another person to attend, speak and vote on matters at the Meeting.

2. Shareholders or their authorized representatives must present the identification documents registered in the shareholder list, the meeting invitation, and the power of attorney (in case the attendee is an authorized representative) to complete the registration procedures for attending the General Meeting.

3. Authorization to attend the AGM:
- A shareholder may authorize an individual or organization (the “Shareholder’s Representative”) to represent all or part of their voting shares.
- The authorization shall be deemed valid and legally effective when all of the following conditions are met:

+ The authorization must be made in writing (in the form provided by the Company or in compliance with applicable laws) and the Company must receive the original power of attorney prior to the shareholder’s attendance at the Meeting or login to the online AGM/electronic voting system.

+ The power of attorney must bear full signatures, clearly stating the full names and, where applicable, the seal (for organizations) of both the principal and the proxy.

+ The shareholder must not have completed login and authentication to attend the AGM on the Electronic Voting System.

- In the event that a shareholder authorizes another person to attend the General Meeting, shareholders are requested to submit a valid power of attorney to the Company no later than 17:30 on April 21, 2026, via one of the following methods:

(1) Send by registered mail/courier or deliver directly to the Company’s address.

(2) Register the authorization on the Online Meeting System at: https://agm.viettelconstruction.com.vn/.

- Shareholders may also send a scanned copy of the power of attorney via email to: ir.viettelconstruction@viettel.com.vn.

- In the event that a shareholder wishes to revoke a previously granted valid authorization, he/she must send a written notice to the Company. The revocation shall take effect from the time the Company receives the original valid written request from the shareholder. The revocation will not be accepted if the notice is received by the Company (via email, postal service, or registered courier) after April 21, 2026.
