2. Method of Attendance and Voting at the General Meeting of Shareholders
a. Forms of attendance and voting
Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives may attend the General Meeting of Shareholders either in person or online and shall exercise their voting rights through Electronic Voting in accordance with the regulations. Detailed instructions for attending the General Meeting of Shareholders are issued by the Company and published on its website at: https://viettelconstruction.com.vn/
b. Voting on matters within the authority of the General Meeting of Shareholders
Voting shall be conducted via Electronic Voting as follows:
- From 08:00 on April 15, 2026, Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives may access the system and vote on matters within the authority of the General Meeting of Shareholders. The closing time for voting shall be announced on the Electronic Voting System or directly during the Meeting. After the voting period ends, the system will no longer record any additional Electronic Voting results.
- Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives shall vote on each agenda item separately. When voting via the Electronic Voting method, for each item, Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives shall select one of the three options: “Approve”, “Disapprove”, or “No Opinion” on the voting ballot available on the Electronic Voting System. Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives shall click “Submit Vote” to save and send their voting results to the system.
- Matters shall be deemed approved when meeting the voting thresholds as prescribed by the Law on Enterprises and the Company’s Charter.
d. Recording and announcement of voting results:
- Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives may change their voting results multiple times but cannot cancel them.
