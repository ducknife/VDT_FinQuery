- Regarding business performance results:
In 2025, the Corporation recorded consolidated revenue of VND 14,057.7 billion, an increase of 11% compared to 2024. Total expenses reached VND 13,312 billion, also increasing by 11%, indicating that costs were effectively controlled in line with revenue scale.
Profit before tax reached VND 745.7 billion, up 11%, while profit after tax reached VND 599.7 billion, up 11.4% year-on-year. The profit growth rate was broadly aligned with revenue growth, reflecting stable operational efficiency.
Despite input cost pressures and competitive market conditions, the Corporation maintained both revenue and profit growth, demonstrating effective execution of business plans and efficient cost management.

- Regarding operational efficiency and liquidity:
The Corporation’s operational efficiency indicators in 2025 showed an improving trend. Return on Assets (ROA) reached 7.8% (compared to 7.6% in 2024), while Return on Equity (ROE) reached 30.5% (compared to 27.9%), indicating improved efficiency in the use of both assets and equity.
Working capital efficiency improved significantly: receivables turnover reached 7.42 times (up from 6.35 times), inventory turnover reached 25.03 times (a strong increase from 16.75 times), and working capital turnover reached 15.54 times (compared to 13.59 times). These indicators reflect strengthened management of receivables and inventories, contributing to enhanced capital utilization efficiency.
Liquidity was maintained at a safe level, with a current ratio of 1.18 times and a quick ratio of 1.11 times, both higher than the previous year and above 1. The debt-to-total-assets ratio stood at 0.75, indicating that the capital structure continues to rely significantly on debt but remains within an appropriate range for the Corporation’s operational characteristics.
Overall, efficiency and liquidity indicators improved compared to 2024. The management of receivables and inventories became more effective, with shorter collection periods, contributing to stronger cash flow and enhanced financial autonomy.

General assessment: In 2025, the Corporation maintained stable growth, with a financial structure appropriate to its operational characteristics. Operational efficiency and liquidity improved, creating a solid foundation for the implementation of business plans in the following years.
