- Upon expiry of the voting period as announced by the Organizing Committee, the Electronic Voting System shall be closed and shall record the most recent voting results of the Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives.
- Voting results shall be determined for each agenda item. The results for each item shall be recorded under the options: “Affirmative”, “Negative” and “Abstention” and shall be calculated as percentages rounded to five (05) decimal places.

**Article 13. Approval of Matters at the General Meeting**

1. The voting ratios required for approving matters submitted to the General Meeting of Shareholders shall comply with the provisions of the Law on Enterprises and the Company’s Charter.

2. For Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives who have logged into the Electronic Voting System but do not submit their voting ballots, they shall be deemed as attending shareholders who do not participate in voting. The number of shares held by such Shareholders/Shareholder Representatives shall not be counted as a basis for determining the voting ratio for matters at the Meeting.

3. The Vote Counting Committee shall be responsible for compiling the voting results based on the records from the Electronic Voting System and preparing the Vote Counting Minutes, which must clearly state the matters approved and the list of elected members for each position. The Vote Counting Minutes shall be signed by the members of the Vote Counting Committee to confirm the accuracy, transparency, and compliance with voting procedures and principles. The Head of the Vote Counting Committee shall announce the Vote Counting Minutes before the General Meeting of Shareholders.

**Article 14. Minutes of the General Meeting of Shareholders**

The proceedings of the General Meeting shall be recorded in Minutes, publicly disclosed, and stored in accordance with applicable laws and the Company’s Charter. The Minutes shall be read before the entire General Meeting and approved prior to the closing of the Meeting.

**CHAPTER IV. EXECUTIVE PROVISIONS**

**Article 15. Effectiveness**

1. These Regulations shall take effect at the 2026 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Company, held on April 22, 2026 when approved by at least 50% of the total voting rights of shareholders present at the opening time.

2. Voting ballots cast through the electronic voting system are only valid when these Regulations takes effect and the General Meeting of Shareholders meets the quorum requirements as stipulated in the Charter.
