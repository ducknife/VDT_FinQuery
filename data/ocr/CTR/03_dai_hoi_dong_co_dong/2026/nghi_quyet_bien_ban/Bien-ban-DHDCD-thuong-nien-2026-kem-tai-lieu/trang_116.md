# REPORT OF THE INDEPENDENT BOARD OF DIRECTORS

To: General Meeting of Shareholders of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation.

The Independent Board Member respectfully report the General Meeting of Shareholders of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation (hereinafter referred to as the “General Meeting”) the 2025 operation results as follows:

- In the final year of the 2021–2025 strategic period of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation, the Executive Board has decisively, closely, and promptly made timely adjustments to the organizational structure and business orientation in order to seize opportunities arising from significant breakthrough changes in the entire political system, while also adapting to market fluctuations and adverse impacts of climate-related natural disasters. As a result, the Corporation successfully completed its 2025 business targets, making significant contributions to the development of the Group’s 5G network infrastructure, and participating in major national key infrastructure projects.

- The Board of Directors has demonstrated a clear strategic leadership role, with well-prepared planning for the 2026–2030 period and large-scale organizational restructuring, thereby establishing a foundation for long-term growth. Supervisory activities have been implemented comprehensively, from the strategic level down to on-site execution, contributing to improved operational efficiency and project quality. The adoption of G20/OECD governance standards, together with the review and refinement of internal regulations, reflects the commitment to enhancing corporate governance standards. The control of related-party transactions has been carried out in accordance with regulations, in a transparent manner and within the scope approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders. However, it is necessary to continue closely monitoring the remediation of issues identified after inspections and supervision in order to further strengthen the sustainability of governance and compliance.

The above is the report of the Independent Board Member send to the General Meeting of Shareholders for consideration and approval. /.
