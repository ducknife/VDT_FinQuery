# Appendix 1. Terminology and abbreviations

| Abbreviations | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| VCC, TCT      | Viettel Construction Joint Stock Company |
| CNCT          | Viettel Construction Branches (Provinces/Cities) |
| LNTT          | Profit before tax |
| LNST          | Profit after tax |
| ROA (%)       | Net profit margin on average total assets |
| ROE (%)       | Net profit margin on average equity |
| ROS (%)       | Net profit margin on total revenue |
| VHKT          | Operations and exploitation |
| NLMT          | Solar energy |
| FT            | Field technical staff |
