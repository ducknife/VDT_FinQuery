# THE EXECUTIVE BOARD’S REPORT ON THE 2025 BUSINESS PERFORMANCE RESULTS AND THE 2026 BUSINESS PLAN

To: General Meeting of Shareholders of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation.

The Executive Board respectfully presents the report on the business performance in 2025 and the business plan for 2026 as follows:

## I. 2025 BUSINESS PERFORMANCE REPORT

### 1. Overview of the business environment in 2025:

In 2025, the global political and economic environment continued to experience complex developments. Geopolitical conflicts persisted in various regions (Russia–Ukraine, Thailand–Cambodia, US–China–Taiwan, etc.). Trade tensions also remained high, particularly following the United States’ tariff policies. According to several reputable organizations, global economic growth is expected to slow down. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) estimate global GDP growth in 2025 at 3.2% while the World Bank (WB) estimates 3.3%.

In this context, Vietnam’s economy continued to grow at a relatively strong pace, with an estimated growth rate of 8.02% compared to 2024, the second highest in the 2011–2025 period. The economy’s scale exceeded USD 514 billion, ranking 32nd globally while GDP per capita reached approximately USD 5,026 per person per year, affirming Vietnam’s position among the fastest-growing economies in the region and the world. Notably, Vietnam has effectively capitalized on global investment shifts. Total registered foreign direct investment (FDI) in Vietnam as of December 31, 2025, reached USD 38.42 billion, up 0.5% year-on-year.

In 2025, Vietnam continued to maintain a stable political environment, providing a solid foundation for economic growth and investment attraction. The reform in streamlining the political system and adjusting administrative boundaries has opened up new development spaces, helping to optimize growth potential.
