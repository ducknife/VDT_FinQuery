- Several key matters were reviewed and approved by the SB, including: the SB’s 2025 work plan; periodic supervisory results; reports and documents submitted to the 2025 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (AGM); the appraisal results for the selection of the independent auditing firm for the 2025 financial year; the set of criteria for evaluating the quality of independent audit services at the Corporation; and conclusions from specialized inspections and supervisory activities conducted by the SB.

- The remuneration and operating expenses of the Supervisory Board (SB) in 2025 have been specified in the Proposal on remuneration of the Board of Directors (BOD) and the SB presented to the General Meeting of Shareholders.

- In 2025, the SB implemented several supervisory activities, including:

  - Supervising the governance and management activities of the Corporation: monitoring the operations of the Board of Directors (BOD) and the Board of Management in implementing resolutions of the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) and the BOD; supervising compliance with laws, the Corporation’s Charter, internal governance regulations, resolutions on delegated authority, and task assignments of the BOD and Executive Board; and assessing the execution of business operations in line with assigned objectives and approved plans.

  - Supervising the financial situation and business performance efficiency: Appraising periodic financial statements (quarterly, semi-annual, and annual) before and after audit; analyzing financial indicators and performance metrics to assess the financial condition, business results, and the Corporation’s ability to achieve its planned targets.

  - Supervising compliance with laws and information disclosure: Monitoring compliance with legal regulations, the Corporation’s Charter, and internal governance regulations in management and administration activities; overseeing compliance with information disclosure obligations and ensuring the legitimate rights and interests of shareholders, the Corporation, and employees.

  - Supervising financial, asset, and cost management: Monitoring and evaluating the management of receivables, inventories, assets, and costs; supervising labor and salary management; and assessing the use of the Corporation’s resources.

  - Supervising investment, procurement, and bidding activities: Overseeing the implementation of investment and procurement activities, bidding processes, contract signing and execution; and reviewing project completion and contract settlement in accordance with regulations.

  - Supervising audit activities and the internal control system: Participating in the selection and quality assessment of the independent audit firm; coordinating with the external auditor during the review and audit of financial statements; supervising the implementation of audit recommendations and conclusions from inspection and audit authorities; and reviewing the implementation of risk management, internal audit activities, and the effectiveness and efficiency of the internal control system.
