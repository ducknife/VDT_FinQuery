7. Monitoring results of the 2025 Annual General Meeting (AGM) Resolutions
- The Board of Directors and the Executive Board have implemented the contents of the 2025 AGM Resolution. Key production and business indicators were all completed and exceeded the approved plan, with higher revenue and profit continuing compared to 2024.
- The Company’s charter capital in 2025 remained unchanged at VND 1,143,858,790,000, equivalent to 114,385,879 shares.
- The distribution of after-tax profit and appropriation to funds was carried out in accordance with the AGM Resolution and applicable laws. The Company paid cash dividends as approved by the AGM at a rate of 21.5% of charter capital, and disclosed information in compliance with legal regulations.
- Independent audit selection: The Corporation organized the selection of an independent auditing firm in compliance with regulations on contractor selection for service procurement. The preparation of the request for proposals and the bid evaluation process were supervised by the Supervisory Board. The Corporation signed Audit Contract No. 210725.001/HDTC.KT6 dated July 21, 2025 with AASC Auditing Firm Limited. The Supervisory Board monitored and supervised the review and audit of the 2025 financial statements. During the process, the audit firm proactively coordinated with the Supervisory Board in accounting issues and the preparation and presentation of the Company’s financial statements, and carried out the review and audit under the signed audit contract.
- Information disclosure was fully and promptly carried out in accordance with securities laws, ensuring transparency for shareholders and investors.
- The payment of remuneration, salaries, and other benefits for members of the Board of Directors, Supervisory Board, and Executive Board was implemented in accordance with the AGM Resolution and the Company’s applicable internal regulations.
