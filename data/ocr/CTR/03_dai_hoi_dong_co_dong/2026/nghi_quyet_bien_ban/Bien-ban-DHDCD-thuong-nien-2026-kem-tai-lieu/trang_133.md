## II. Proposal for approval of contracts and transactions with Viettel Group in 2027

### Contracts and transactions with a value of less than 35% of the total asset value according to the latest financial statements.

Contracts and transactions of a regular and recurring nature throughout the 12 months of the year, each having a value of less than 35% of the total asset value, make it impracticable to convene a General Meeting of Shareholders to approve each individual contract or transaction. Therefore, the Executive Board respectfully submits this proposal to the Board of Directors for consideration and the General Meeting of Shareholders for approval of a general policy allowing the Corporation to enter into all contracts and transactions with a value of less than 35% of the total asset value, with similar content and scope of work, as follows:

- **a) Counterparties with which the Corporation conducts transactions**: Viettel Group and branches of Viettel Group or companies over which Viettel Group has controlling influence through share ownership, capital contribution, or decision-making authority.

- **b) Main contents of the contracts and transactions**: Construction, repair, reinforcement, relocation, and refurbishment of telecommunications infrastructure; emergency telecommunications response; construction, installation, reinforcement, maintenance, repair, dismantling and relocation of station sites and related materials and equipment; civil construction, warehouse repair and renovation; infrastructure leasing for station locations; trading of goods and telecommunications equipment, electronics, household electrical appliances, and accessories; provision and procurement of network operational services; design consultancy services; advertising and media services; goods transportation services; and other goods and services.

- **c) Value of each contract/transaction**: Each contract or transaction shall have a value of less than 35% of the Corporation’s total assets as recorded in the most recent financial statements.

- **d) Term for entering into contracts and transactions**: From January 1, 2027 to December 31, 2027.
