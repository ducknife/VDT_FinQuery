As of December 31, 2025, the Corporation’s total assets reached VND 8,185.5 billion, an increase of 15.2% compared to the beginning of the year. The asset scale continued to expand, meeting the requirements of business growth.

- Regarding the structure of assets and capital sources:
The asset structure shifted toward a higher proportion of current assets. Specifically, current assets reached VND 6,625.4 billion, accounting for approximately 80.9% of total assets, and increased by 22.2% compared to the beginning of the year. The main growth driver came from cash and short-term financial investments (up 90.9%), reflecting the Corporation’s proactive cash flow management and maintenance of high liquidity. At the same time, receivables and inventories were well controlled, helping to reduce working capital pressure.

Long-term assets reached VND 1,560.1 billion, decreasing by 7.4%, reflecting a shift from the investment phase to the operational phase, as several projects have been completed and begun generating revenue. This structure is consistent with the Corporation’s operational characteristics and business cycle.
