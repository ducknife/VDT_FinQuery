# PROPOSAL TO THE GENERAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS
Re: Approval of remuneration and compensation for the Board of Directors and the Supervisory Board in 2025 and proposed remuneration for 2026

To: General Meeting of Shareholders of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation.

Pursuant to the Charter of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation;  
Based on the business performance in 2025 and the business plan for 2026, the Board of Directors respectfully submits to the General Meeting of Shareholders for approval of the remuneration for the Board of Directors and the Supervisory Board as follows:

## 1. Final settlement of remuneration and salaries for the Board of Directors and the Supervisory Board in 2025:

### 1.1. Board of Directors:
The total remuneration and salary for 2025 is VND 6.61 billion, of which:

- **Remuneration for non-executive members of the Board of Directors:**
  - Remuneration for independent Board members: VND 22 million/person/month.
  - Remuneration for other Board members: VND 7 million/person/month.

- **Salary for executive members of the Board of Directors:**
  Payment is made in accordance with the Corporation’s salary regulations and the Resolution approved by the 2025 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders.

### 1.2. Supervisory Board:
The total remuneration and salary for 2025 is VND 2.26 billion, of which:

- **Remuneration for non-executive members of the Supervisory Board:**
  None.

- **Salary for executive members of the Supervisory Board:**
  Payment is made in accordance with the Corporation’s salary regulations and the Resolution approved by the 2025 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders.

*(The above figures do not include bonuses from the bonus fund for Managers as approved by the 2025 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders.)*
