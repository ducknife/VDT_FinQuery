- Gradually increased the proportion of revenue from new business segments and external customers. All new business segments recorded double-digit growth compared to 2024. Revenue from external customers grew by 15% year-on-year, with its share increasing from 39.7% in 2024 to 41% in 2025.
- Digital transformation activities continued to be strongly implemented, reaching a level of 4.16/5 by year-end with all 6/6 areas achieving scores of 4 or higher (Advanced level).

**3.2. Risks and weaknesses to be improved**

- The construction segment did not meet planned indicators due to a decline in public investment projects during the transition to a two-tier local government system, along with the merger of provinces, agencies and units. Many construction projects were delayed or stalled due to issues related to licensing procedures, site clearance, etc., leading to extended implementation timelines.
- The M&E (Mechanical & Electrical) segment did not achieve expected growth. The main reason was the slow recovery of retail consumer demand, especially for non-essential products. In addition, the pipeline of M&E projects funded by public investment was negatively affected in a similar manner to the construction segment.
