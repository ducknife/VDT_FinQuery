**4.1.1. On-site supervision:**
- To prepare for new business sectors and growth opportunities in the coming period, the Chairman of the Board of Directors conducted several activities and thematic studies, including surveying, seeking cooperation opportunities, and researching the market in the field of social housing investment in Phu Tho and Bac Ninh, among others.
- Conducting field assessments of planning and construction execution processes, as well as project organization procedures for B2B construction projects, and evaluating schedule control and construction quality of projects in Hung Yen province.
- Carrying out on-site supervision of operational activities to strengthen and improve network quality as well as the completion and settlement of telecommunications projects in Quang Ninh province.
- Researching and exchanging practical experience with relevant units on corporate governance principles under G20/OECD standards in order to make a plan on improving Viettel Construction’s corporate governance score.

In 2025, the Board of Directors reviewed and re-issued five regulations in the financial sector to adjust their contents in line with the current management and governance practices of the Corporation.

**4.1.4. Supervision of related-party transactions:**
In 2025, 5,083 contracts with related parties were signed (all contracts had a value of less than 35% of total assets and were approved by the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders in 2024; no contracts of more than 35% of total assets were recorded). The total value of these contracts was VND 3,629 billion. All related-party transactions were contracts/transactions signed with Viettel Group (Military Industry and Telecoms Group) and its subsidiaries. No transactions were conducted with related parties of other entities.
