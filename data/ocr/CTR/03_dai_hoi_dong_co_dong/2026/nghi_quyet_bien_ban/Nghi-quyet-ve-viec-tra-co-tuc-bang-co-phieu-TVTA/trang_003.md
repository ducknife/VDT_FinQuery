DECIDES:

Article 1. To approve the share issuance plan for dividend payment as
follows:

- Share name: Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation
- Type of shares: Common shares
- Par value: VND 10,000 per share
- Total number of outstanding shares: 114,385,879 shares
- Number of shares to be issued: 13,726,305 shares (equivalent to 12% of
the total outstanding shares)
- Exercise ratio: 100:12 (for every 100 shares held by a shareholder on the
final registration date, the shareholder will receive 12 new shares)
- Source of issuance: Undistributed profit after tax
- Eligible participants: Existing shareholders whose names are on the
shareholder list as of the final registration date for entitlement
- Final registration date: The General Meeting of Shareholders authorizes
the Board of Directors to determine an appropriate time based on market
conditions and the Corporation’s operations, in compliance with applicable laws.
