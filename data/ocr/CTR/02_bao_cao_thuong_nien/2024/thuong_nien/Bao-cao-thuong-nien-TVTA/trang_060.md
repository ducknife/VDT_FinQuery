# II. ESTABLISHMENT AND DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation was established on October 30, 1995, as an independently operated entity and an indispensable part of the ecosystem of the Military Industry and Telecoms Group (Viettel Group). With a workforce of up to 11,000 personnel and an operational network spanning all 63 provinces and cities across Vietnam, Viettel Construction stands as Vietnam’s leading technical service provider, operating on the foundation of six core strategic business pillars: Infrastructure Investment; Construction (including telecommunications infrastructure and both B2C and B2B civil construction); Information Technology; Integrated Solutions; Technical Services; and Operation and Maintenance.

To date, after nearly 30 years of operation, Viettel Construction has built a comprehensive telecommunications infrastructure system across Vietnam, with more than 50,000 broadcast stations and 320,000 kilometers of fiber optic cable - equivalent to eight times the Earth’s circumference - covering 100% of the national territory, including coastal islands and remote mountainous areas. At the same time, Viettel Construction has also participated in the construction of telecommunications infrastructure in 10 countries across Asia, Africa, and the Americas.

## 1995 – 2003

- Establishment of the Survey and Design Enterprise and the Construction Enterprise (predecessor of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation), under the Military Telecommunications Electronics Company.
- The enterprise executed foundation construction and antenna tower installation for high-quality domestic and international projects, including: the 120-meter antenna tower for Tuyen Quang Television Station, the 140-meter antenna tower for Lao National Television, and various towers for Lao Telecom in Vientiane.

## 2004 – 2009

- The Construction Enterprise was renamed Viettel Construction Company.
- In 2006, the company was converted from a dependent-accounting entity to an independently-accounting entity, with a new business name: Viettel Construction One Member Limited Liability State Company.
- In December 2007, the company began implementing network construction in the Kingdom of Cambodia.

## 2010 – 2017

- In 2010, Viettel Construction focused heavily on the construction of 3G network infrastructure. The 2G and 3G networks of Viettel Group became the largest networks in Vietnam.
- On January 15, 2010, the Minister of National Defense issued a decision to convert Viettel Construction One Member Limited Liability State Company into Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation.
- In 2011, Viettel Construction organized deployment teams to carry out construction projects in Haiti.
- In 2014, the company established three subsidiaries in overseas markets: Cameroon, Burundi, and Tanzania.
- In 2015, it completed licensing procedures with the Ministry of Planning and Investment to operate in Laos and Myanmar.

## 2018 – 2020

- Became Vietnam’s leading provider of technical services based on digital platforms.
- Officially operated based on five key business pillars: Infrastructure Investment for Leasing, Civil Construction, Information Technology, Integrated Solutions, and Operation & Maintenance.
- In 2020, the company recorded VND 6,381 billion in revenue (equivalent to 106% of the plan) and VND 274 billion in profit (equivalent to 136% of the plan).

## 2021 – 2025

- Became an investor, operator, and provider of high-efficiency, smart infrastructure projects.
- In 2024, Viettel Construction achieved VND 12,663 billion in revenue, maintaining its position as Vietnam’s No.1 TowerCo.
- Target revenue by 2025 is set to reach VND 13,968 billion.
- Aims to achieve Digital Maturity Level 5/5 according to TM Forum standards by 2025.
