# 2. SUB-COMMITTEES OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ SUB-COMMITTEES**: Secretariat

The Secretariat supports the BoD in organizing BoD meetings and the AGMS. It ensures that activities such as information disclosure are conducted in strict compliance with legal regulations, the Company Charter, and internal governance policies. The Secretariat also ensures that all information provided is appropriate, complete, accurate, and timely.

In addition, the Secretariat Sub-Committee is responsible for managing the IR function of the Corporation. The IR department of VCC is headed by Mr. Phạm Hồng Quân, Chief of the Secretariat.
