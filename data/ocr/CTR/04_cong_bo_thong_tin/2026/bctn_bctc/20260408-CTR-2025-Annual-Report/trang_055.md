## EXPERIENCE AND SUMMARY OF BACKGROUND:

### MR. DO MANH HUNG
- 28 years of experience in the field of electronics and telecommunications.
- He has held several key positions in various companies, including: Deputy General Director of Viettel Network Corporation; General Director and Member of the Board of Directors of Viettel Global Investment Joint Stock Corporation (VTG); Chairman of Viettel Import-Export and Trading One Member Limited Company.

### MR. PHAM DINH TRUONG
- 22 years of experience in the field of electronics and telecommunications.
- He has held several key positions at various companies, including: General Director of Viettel Network Corporation; Deputy General Director of Viettel Telecommunications Corporation.

### MS. VU THI MAI
- 22 years of experience in business administration and human resource management.
- She has held various positions, including: Lecturer at the Academy of Finance; Head of International Cooperation Department at BIDV; Deputy Director of Viettel Academy; positions at Viettel Group’s Human Resources Organization Department; and currently Head of the Human Resources Organization Department at Viettel Group.

### MR. NGUYEN TAT TRUONG
- 23 years of experience in electronics and engineering.
- He has held various positions at Viettel Network Corporation, including: Head of Technical Department; Head of Internal Control Department.

### MR. NGUYEN QUANG KHAI
- 28 years of experience in the field of Information Technology.
- He has held several key positions in various companies, including: Solution Consulting Director at Oracle Vietnam; Chief Architect at SAP Vietnam; Deputy Chief Technology Officer at Adayroi – Vingroup; Chief Executive Officer and Member of the Board of Directors at DMSpro.
