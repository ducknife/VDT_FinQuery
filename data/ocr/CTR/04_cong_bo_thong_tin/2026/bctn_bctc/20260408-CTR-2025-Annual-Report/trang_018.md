# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## VII. RISK MANAGEMENT

Entering 2025, in the context that a global economy still faces multidimensional volatility (wars, rapid AI technological breakthroughs, price fluctuations, etc.), the Corporation has proactively transformed challenges into opportunities through a flexible risk management strategy and sustainable growth approach.

Risk management is implemented as a core capability, integrated throughout all business operations—from strategic planning, business planning and project implementation to the management of major changes such as organizational restructuring, policy adjustments, process improvements, and technology system upgrades.

In 2025, the Risk Management System has reached maturity level 5.

The Risk Management System at the Corporation is standardized according to the Three-Line Defense Model, establishing a robust, comprehensive and proactive governance structure. This model is not only a control tool but also a foundation for protecting the company's value against complicated market fluctuations. Specifically:

The first line of defense consists of risk owners, including the Business Centers, operational Division and 34 provincial/city branches under the Corporation. These are the forces directly involved in generating and interacting with risks in daily production and business operations, responsible for complying with the Corporation's procedures and regulations and managing risks within their assigned functions. These units are responsible for proactively identifying, assessing and controlling risks within their operational processes, ensuring that all transactions and projects are implemented within acceptable risk limits.

The second line of defense is led by the Legal & Internal Control Department, in coordination with specialized operational units. This line is responsible for designing and operating the Corporation's risk management framework through the issuance of policies, regulations, key risk indicators (KRIs) and quantitative and digital risk assessment tools. Simultaneously, Line 2 performs the function of independent review, monitoring the implementation of Line 1, giving early warning of systemic risks, and advising the Executive Board to ensure consistency and effectiveness throughout the system.

The third line of defense is Internal Audit under the Board of Directors, which provides the highest level of independent and objective assurance regarding the effectiveness and efficiency of the two preceding lines of defense. Line 3 does not participate in operation or policy development, but focuses on assessment and verification through risk-based audits; thereby verifying the level of compliance, assessing the reliability of the governance framework, and recommending strategic improvements to enhance the overall risk management capacity of the Corporation. The three-tiered operating model at the Corporation creates a proactive mechanism for controlling and adapting to market fluctuations. This helps the Corporation not only defend against financial, legal, and operational risks but also strengthen sustainable development and realize its goal of becoming the most efficient investor, operator, and provider of smart infrastructure by 2030.
