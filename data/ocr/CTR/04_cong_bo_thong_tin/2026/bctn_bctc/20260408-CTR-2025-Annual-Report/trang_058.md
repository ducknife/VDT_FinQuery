# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## ① Assessment of the Board of Directors during the year

### General assessment of the Board of Directors’ activities

- In 2025, the Board of Directors (BOD) of the Corporation carried out various supervisory activities in line with the actual operations of the enterprise, including: reviewing, assessing and developing the 2026–2030 strategy toward the objectives approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS); formulating a human resources strategy to support business operations for the 2026–2030 period on the principle that “Viettel Construction is a comprehensive standardized human resource ecosystem and a benchmark model for Vietnamese enterprises”; restructuring the organizational model of the Corporation by merging 63 branches into 34 branches and terminating the operations of 29 branches (in line with the merger of provincial/city administrative units); supervising the profit distribution of subsidiaries; and implementing and monitoring the execution of resolutions of the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders, including business plan implementation, dividend payment, related-party transactions and approval of independent auditors).

- On a quarterly basis, the BOD conducts assessments of business performance and financial efficiency of the units. The BOD assigns quarterly KPI indicators covering business performance, efficiency and operational indicators to ensure cost optimization and improved capital utilization efficiency.

- Every six months or annually, the BOD reviews the implementation results of strategic plans (including business strategy, investment strategy, and capital strategy for the five-year period), thereby making appropriate adjustments when necessary. The capital strategy is evaluated based on capital structure, debt structure, capital utilization, business strategy and the Corporation’s risk appetite.

### IN ADDITION, THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS ALSO CARRIED OUT A NUMBER OF THEMATIC TASKS AND ON-SITE SUPERVISORY ACTIVITIES AS FOLLOWS:

#### On-site supervision:

- In preparation for new business lines and growth spaces for the coming period, the Chairman of the Board of Directors conducted a number of activities/thematic studies, including: surveying, seeking cooperation opportunities, and conducting market research in the social housing investment sector in Phu Tho and Bac Ninh provinces, etc.

- Conducted field visits to assess the planning and construction implementation processes, construction organization procedures for B2B construction projects and to evaluate the progress control and quality of construction works in Hung Yen province.

- Performed on-site supervision of operational activities to strengthen and improve network quality as well as the completion and settlement of telecommunications projects in Quang Ninh province.

- Conducted research and practical discussions on corporate governance principles with relevant units under the G20/OECD framework in order to develop a plan on improving VCC’s corporate governance score.

#### Supervision of the implementation of corrective actions for issues identified in inspection conclusions

#### Supervision of legal updates and revisions to internal regulations:

- In 2025, the Board of Directors reviewed and re-issued five regulations in the financial sector to update and align their contents with the current practical management and governance requirements of the Corporation.

#### Supervision of related-party transactions:

- In 2025, a total of 5,083 contracts with related parties were executed (all of which were contracts with a value of less than 35% of total assets and had been approved in principle at the 2024 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders; no contracts of over 35% of total assets were incurred). The total value of the signed contracts was VND 2,158.9 billion. All related-party transactions were contracts/transactions entered into with Viettel Group – Military Industry and Telecoms Group and its member units, and no transactions were recorded with other unrelated related parties.

## ② Assessment of individual members’ performance

Members of the Board of Directors fully attended all Board meetings and participated in written opinion consultations with a high sense of responsibility for the best interests of shareholders and the sustainable development of the Company.

In addition to fulfilling the general responsibilities of the Board of Directors, each member has successfully completed their assigned duties as follows:

- The Chairman of the Board of Directors fulfilled his duties in accordance with the provisions of enterprise law and the Company Charter in convening and organizing annual work programs, assigning responsibilities among members and organizing meetings and written consultations to issue resolutions in compliance with legal regulations. The Chairman also provided strategic orientation and review for the Corporation. He led market research activities to explore new business areas and spearheaded studies on corporate governance principles in line with international best practices, aiming to enhance corporate governance beyond compliance and position the Company among the leading enterprises in Vietnam in terms of governance quality.

- Other members of the Board of Directors have successfully completed their assigned tasks, providing timely and creative contributions to the Board’s management and governance activities, thereby contributing to the excellent achievement of the 2025 business plan.
