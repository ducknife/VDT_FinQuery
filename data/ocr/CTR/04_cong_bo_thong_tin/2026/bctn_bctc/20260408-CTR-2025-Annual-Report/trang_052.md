▶ VCC respects the legitimate rights of its creditors and is committed to ensuring that these rights are properly safeguarded. Depending on specific circumstances, as well as negotiation requirements and outcomes with creditors, VCC takes one or more of the following security measures:
- Use of VCC-owned assets as collateral to secure obligations.
- Provision of unsecured guarantees or other forms of security as agreed with creditors.

- Periodic reporting on the implementation of security terms and disclosure of information about creditors’ rights assurance measures.

**5. For customers:**

▶ Protection of Customer Rights: VCC consistently listens to customer feedback and contributions through various customer satisfaction assessment and survey programs, implemented via diverse and convenient channels such as online surveys and telephone interviews. In 2025, Viettel Construction recorded 28,703 customers participating in satisfaction surveys, of which 94.6% expressed satisfaction with the quality of products and services provided by the Company. In addition, the Company conducted surveys on customers’ willingness to recommend its products and services after usage. Accordingly, 6,819 respondents participated in this survey, with 89.8% indicating that they would recommend the Company’s products and services to friends and relatives. In 2025, 1,664 customer support feedback cases were received, and 100% of these cases were acknowledged and resolved in a timely manner.

▶ Marketing and Trademark: VCC strictly complies with applicable laws and standards relating to trademarks, products, services, and marketing communication activities. Currently, VCC has not recorded any complaints related to product or service trademarks and has not been involved in any infringement of intellectual property rights of other entities. The Corporation has issued guidelines for implementing promotional programs and conducts periodic reviews of all promotional activities carried out during the year. In addition, VCC’s trademark portfolio has been officially registered with the Intellectual Property Office of Viet Nam to ensure transparent and formal protection of its brand rights.

▶ Information Security: VCC is committed to ensuring the protection and confidentiality of customer information in full compliance with applicable laws and safeguarding customer rights and interests. The Corporation has issued a Personal Data Protection Policy in accordance with Government Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP dated April 14, 2023. Accordingly, the policy clearly defines the purposes of personal data usage, data processing methods, as well as the rights and obligations of customers (including Decision No. 6796/QD-VCC on the issuance of Personal Data Protection Regulations at Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation, Announcement No. 6862/TB-VCC on the implementation of the Personal Data Protection Policy for customers via websites and sales applications, and Announcement No. 606/TB-VCC on the application of hotline numbers in VCC contracts).

**6. For shareholders:**

▶ VCC shareholders are always treated equitably, with their rights and interests fully protected at the highest level. The Corporation is committed to continuously enhancing value creation and maximizing benefits for its shareholders.
