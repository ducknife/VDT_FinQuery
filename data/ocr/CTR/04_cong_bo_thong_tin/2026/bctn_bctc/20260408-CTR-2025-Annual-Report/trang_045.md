# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## 5. Key Initiatives by Business Segment

### ① Operations and Exploitation

**Enhancing the quality of Operations and Exploitation (O&M) workforce**

- Workforce planning and management to ensure adequate technical staffing levels:
  - Calculate staffing norms down to the Technical Center level to ensure workforce continuity and improve labor productivity.
  - Ensure staffing levels are aligned with approved norms: apply RPA solutions to automatically generate daily alerts to O&E management on workforce surpluses or shortages at the Technical Center level, enabling timely adjustments.
- Strengthen training programs for O&E technical personnel: Implement structured training programs, including onboarding programs for new field technicians (FTs) and targeted training courses for underperforming FTs.

**Application of digital transformation in Operations and Exploitation (O&E)**

- Apply AI in analyzing site images to monitor progress and ensure the quality of O&E activities.
- Apply RPA to support automated alerts for workforce surpluses or shortages against approved staffing norms

**Strengthening network resilience and sustainability**

- Proactively reinforce critical points in the transmission network (approximately 5,000 points per quarter).
- Coordinate with VTNet to mitigate the impact of storms on the transmission infrastructure in accordance with VTNet’s plans (after each storm, jointly report damages, conduct field surveys, develop solutions, and implement action plans).

---

### ② Infrastructure-for-lease Investment

Accelerate the identification and promotion of renewable energy investment opportunities: Strengthen project sourcing and engagement starting from the Branch level, focusing on large customers with high solar energy demand such as factories, industrial parks, export processing zones, government agencies, schools, hospitals with energy-saving budgets, and agricultural farms. In parallel, assess feasibility and promote the implementation of wind power projects.

Expand collaborator and partner channels for co-location development at BTS sites: Assign Work Orders (WO) to provincial units to engage and work with telecom operators, monitor progress and evaluate implementation results. Designate Branch Directors as focal points to directly and consistently engage with telecom operators such as VNPT (Vietnam Posts and Telecommunications Group) and MobiFone Corporation within their respective areas of responsibility.

---

### ③ B2B Construction

**Sales channels**

- Strengthen engagement with local authorities and actively pursue public investment projects at the Branch level.
- Develop a portfolio of strategic clients in the real estate sector:
  - Establish joint ventures/consortia with reputable construction and MEP companies to enhance capabilities and meet eligibility requirements for large-scale tenders.
  - Increase engagement and sign strategic cooperation agreements with existing investors/clients who have previously partnered with VCC, thereby increasing the proportion of repeated sales.
  - Build and expand sales channels and project access networks for FDI projects at key branches through investment consulting firms, Departments of Planning and Investment, and Management Boards of industrial parks/export processing zones in provinces/cities.
- Explore opportunities to implement construction projects in international markets, particularly in markets where the Group has already invested or established representative offices.

**Execution and control channel:**

- Build a core team at the Center level with sufficient capability to deliver national key projects and FDI projects:
  - construction communities (e.g., Facebook groups).
  - Contact recruitment networks within leading construction companies (such as Cotecons Construction Joint Stock Company, Hoa Binh Construction Group, and Central Construction Joint Stock Company, etc.) to identify potential candidates.
- Strengthen and standardize Project Management Boards (PMBs) at branch level.
- Research and apply green materials in VCC construction projects to build a "green contractor" image and enhance capability to participate in FDI projects.
- Enhance management and control through a three-tier structure: Center – Branch – Project Management Board; strengthen the application of digital technologies in site supervision and reporting.
