# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## ③ Partner Experience

### ▶ Objectives and orientation:
To elevate the relationship from a “pure outsourcing model” to a “strategic partnership,” positioning partners as an extended arm representing VCC’s core capabilities.

### ▶ Key activities and achievements:
- Issued the partner journey framework as a guiding principle for all related activities.
- Developed a partner management system/software.
- Established partner segmentation and sizing.

### ▶ Roadmap:
In 2026, Partner Experience management maturity is targeted to reach Level 4.0+/5.

### ▶ Partner and channel management program:
The 2026 partner and channel management program is designed based on a 7-step execution checklist: Stakeholders → Required behaviors → Embedding behaviors into workflows → Data → Cross-checking → GAP identification (actual vs. design) → Operational snapshot (Reporting – KPI), aligned with defined touchpoints of each program and key KPIs derived from designed behaviors.

## ④ Customer-centric Culture

### ▶ Objectives and orientation:
- A customer-centric culture is a core capability of Customer Experience (CX) management. This culture must be deeply embedded and internalized in the subconscious behaviors of employees, reflected through key development pillars: people, processes, tools, and governance. Embedding customer-centric behaviors goes beyond awareness and outward actions toward customers; it requires deeper development in both cognition and behavior, focusing on customers’ emotions and perceptions.

### ▶ Specific target indicators:
- Business performance metrics: CSAT across all business areas targeted at 99%; reduction in customer complaints (PAKH): Technical Services (1.2%), Integrated Solutions (1.4%), Construction B2C (1%).
- Revenue growth from customer referrals: Revenue generated from referred customers targeted to grow by 238% compared to 2025.

### ▶ Processes and regulations:
- Service quality measurement and survey process
- Customer feedback/complaint handling process
- Operational action plan to improve Customer Experience maturity score in 2026

### ▶ Implementation approach and 2026 roadmap:
- Customer insights: Conduct research and development of customer personas for 2026, reassessing customer touchpoints and current perceptions. The research will be carried out using both qualitative methods (in-depth interviews) and quantitative methods (large-scale surveys).
  → Further develop customer personas and journeys for elevator, fire protection systems and high-value customer segments contributing significantly to the Corporation.
- Customer/partner listening: Develop appreciation initiatives (e.g., gifts and recognition programs) for customers/partners who provide valuable feedback, supporting VCC in improving processes and service quality.
- Customer/partner engagement and appreciation: gift and appreciation activities on special occasions, combined with customer outreach and appreciation efforts to build a high-quality referral customer base and foster long-term partnerships.
- Communication and advocacy: Develop programs and mini-games to encourage customers to share their experiences when using products and services, while promoting the benefits of customers when successfully referring new customers to VCC.
