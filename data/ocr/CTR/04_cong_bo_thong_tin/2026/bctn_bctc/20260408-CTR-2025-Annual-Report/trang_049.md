# I. ASSESSMENT OF THE CORPORATION'S OPERATING PERFORMANCE

## 1. Overall Assessment

The global political environment remained complicated, with ongoing conflicts in multiple regions and U.S. tax policies creating barriers to global trade. Domestically, GDP recorded strong growth of over 8%, with a stable macroeconomic environment. The Party and Government issued numerous resolutions and legal documents to support economic growth.

2025 was marked by unpredictable natural disasters and weather conditions, placing significant pressure on network infrastructure and the Corporation's business operations. At the same time, the year featured several major national events such as A50, A80, and Party Congresses at various levels. As a leading unit responsible for deploying technical infrastructure to ensure connectivity for these events, this represented both a challenge and a source of pride for the Corporation's employees.

Despite these challenges, the Corporation successfully fulfilled its business plan as approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders and maintained double-digit growth.

In terms of corporate governance, the Corporation promptly restructured its provincial branch model in alignment with administrative unit consolidation at the provincial/city level, while implementing strategic business directions and developing resource strategies for the 2026–2030 period, with a focus on high growth and sustainable development.

## 2. Assessment of the Implementation of Tasks Assigned by the General Meeting of Shareholders

### ① Production and Business Results:

#### Business Results in 2025

- Consolidated revenue in 2025 reached VND 14,058 billion, achieving 101% of the plan and increasing by 11% year-on-year compared to 2024.
- Consolidated profit after tax (PAT) in 2025 reached VND 599.7 billion, achieving 107% of the plan and increasing by 11.4% year-on-year compared to 2024.
- Parent Company: Total revenue reached VND 11,934 billion, achieving 97% of the plan and increasing by 17.7% year-on-year. Profit after tax (PAT) reached VND 587.4 billion, achieving 112% of the plan and increasing by 10.5% year-on-year. Return on Equity (ROE) reached 31.1%, achieving 125% of the plan.

#### Implementation Results of Key Strategic Directions and Priorities:

The Corporation has effectively implemented the strategic directions and key tasks assigned as follows:

- The Corporation has completed the development of its Business Strategy for the 2026–2030 period with key objectives including: becoming one of the Top 100 largest enterprises in Vietnam (VNR500 ranking); providing a fully integrated ecosystem covering Consulting – Design – Implementation – Operation – Optimization – Investment; achieving Top 1–3 market position in each business segment nationwide and at provincial level across four core business pillars; reaching total revenue of VND 25,071 billion by 2030 with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 12% during the period; and achieving profit before tax of VND 1,050 billion by 2030 with a CAGR of 7.5%.

- The Corporation has expanded its business beyond the Group with revenue from non-Viettel partners reaching VND 5,804 billion, accounting for 42% of total Corporation revenue.

- The Corporation continues to affirm its position as the No.1 tower company (TowerCo) in Vietnam and aims to be among the Top 10 TowerCos in Asia. In 2025, the Corporation added 2,000 BTS leasing stations, bringing the cumulative total to 12,000 BTS sites owned; 2.45 million m² of DAS; 2,716 km of transmission infrastructure; and 16.92 MWp of rooftop solar power. Overseas business operations have been deployed in a synchronized and effective manner in line with the business model in Vietnam. Two core markets are developing business activities similar to the parent company, and the Corporation has also expanded into additional international markets.

- System governance and management capabilities have been strengthened, with the following maturity levels achieved: Digital maturity score of 4.05/5; Corporate culture maturity of 5/5; Risk management maturity of 5/5; and Quality management system maturity of 3/3.

### ② Implementation of matters authorized by the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS)

#### Selection of the audit firm for the 2025 financial statements:

The Board of Directors/Supervisory Board organized and approved the selection of AASC Auditing Firm Company Limited as the external audit firm for the Company’s 2025 financial statements.

#### Dividend payment for 2024 in 2025:

The Board of Directors implemented the Resolution No. 37/NQ-VCC dated September 11, 2025 regarding the cash dividend payment for the 2024 fiscal year at a rate of 21.5%, in accordance with the Annual General Meeting of Shareholders’ resolution and ensuring compliance with applicable legal requirements. The final registration date for dividend entitlement was October 01, 2025, and the payment date was October 15, 2025.

#### Related-party transactions:

In 2025, a total of 5,083 contracts with related parties were executed. All contracts had a value of less than 35% of total assets, with no contracts exceeding the 35% threshold, and were conducted in accordance with the submission approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders concerning related-party transactions with Vietnam Military Industry and Telecoms Group (Viettel Group). The total value of signed contracts amounted to VND 3,629.1 billion. During the year, no transactions were recorded with other related parties beyond those specified above.
