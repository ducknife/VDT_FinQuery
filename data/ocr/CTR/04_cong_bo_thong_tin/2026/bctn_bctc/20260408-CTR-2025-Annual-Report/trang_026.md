# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## 2. Results and Improvements

### BUSINESS

- Operation & Exploitation: ~63,498 BTS stations; 8.15 million CDBR subscribers.
- New construction of 2,000 BTS stations, achieving ~100% of the annual plan; cumulative total reaching 12,000 BTS stations by the end of 2025, positioning VCC as the No.1 TowerCo in Vietnam.
- During the year, VCC completed construction of 1,720 turnkey houses for customers; cumulative total reached 5,590 houses. Individual housing projects have covered 71% of communes/wards (~2,353/3,321), representing the largest market coverage in Vietnam's construction sector.
- Deployment of solar energy systems for more than 10,000 enterprise and household customers, with a total installed capacity of 166 MWp, an increase of 205% compared to 2024.

### PROCESS

Continuous improvement to ensure flexibility in adapting to system requirements and each stage of the Corporation's development.

- In 2025, 47/47 processes were successfully improved; 46/47 processes achieved a reduction in execution time of approximately 34.4% compared to before implementation.
- Process digitalization rate: Reached 91%, equivalent to 107% of the annual target (85%); Process automation rate: Reached 61%, equivalent to 101% of the annual target (60%).

### PEOPLE

Developed a talent management strategy, identifying and planning 389 talents, equivalent to ~3.75% of the total workforce. Of which: 23 strategic talents (~0.22%), 63 expert talents (~0.61%), and 303 operational talents (~2.92%).

### TECHNOLOGY

In 2025, VCC implemented 18 AI use cases, achieving 150% of the annual target; cumulative total reached 38 AI use cases, covering 100% of departments and centers across the Corporation.

### GOVERNANCE

- Performance-based management: 100% of employees are assigned, evaluated, and compensated based on KPI performance results.
- Culture-based management: 100% of key dimensions met 2025 targets (Corporate Culture maturity: 5/5; Risk Management maturity: 5/5; Quality Management maturity: 4/target 4; Partner Management maturity: 3.61/target 3.61; Customer Responsibility maturity: 4.3/4.25).
