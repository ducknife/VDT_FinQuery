## 2. Risk Management Policy

The Risk Management Policy (RMP) of VCC defines the principles, requirements, and responsibilities for risk management and fraud prevention across the entire Corporation. It serves as the legal basis for the CEO and subordinate units to implement control measures, ensuring consistent application in alignment with VCC’s mission, vision, core values, and strategic objectives.

The RMP is reviewed, assessed, and updated periodically on an annual or quarterly basis, as well as on an ad-hoc basis when there are significant changes in the business environment, legal framework, development strategy or other factors that may impact VCC’s operations. This ensures the Corporation’s ability to adapt flexibly to market fluctuations and minimize emerging risks.

## 3. Risk Reporting

The Corporation’s Risk Management reporting system is operated on a monthly basis through an internal control software platform, providing the Executive Board with key KPIs/KRIs to monitor compliance levels, control effectiveness and risk exposure across Construction Branches. Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) are automatically calculated by the system, ensuring timely, objective, and consistent data. The system also displays alerts using a Green – Yellow – Red threshold mechanism. When KRIs exceed predefined thresholds, the system automatically triggers requirements for the development and implementation of response measures to bring risk levels back within acceptable limits. The reports focus on KRI trend movements, threshold breaches, root causes and corrective actions taken by units. This supports timely decision-making by the Executive Board, strengthens self-inspection and self-monitoring at the operational level and ensures safe and sustainable business growth.

## 4. Key Risks

### FINANCIAL RISK

Financial risk management at VCC is implemented in a synchronized and systematic manner to ensure cash flow safety and effective control of liquidity risks, receivables, costs and financial fluctuations across the entire Corporation. This activity is integrated throughout the entire process—from financial planning, budget management and project execution to monitoring, debt collection, and final settlement—thereby maintaining payment capacity and financial stability under all market conditions.

VCC applies a system of financial risk indicators (KRIs) that are automatically calculated on the management software platform, enabling continuous monitoring of early warning signals such as cash flow fluctuations, overdue receivables ratio, credit limit utilization and cost deviations against planned budgets. When KRIs exceed defined thresholds, the system automatically requires relevant units to develop and implement timely response measures to bring risks back within acceptable levels, thereby supporting proactive decision-making by the Executive Board and ensuring safe and sustainable financial growth.

The Corporation regularly reviews and updates its framework for evaluating project owners’ financial capacity and issues standardized guidelines for Construction Branches in contract negotiation and signing processes, thereby minimizing credit and payment risks from the outset. In parallel, VCC accelerates digital transformation by developing a real-time project progress and cash flow tracking system, allowing early identification and warning of financial risks during project implementation.

Responsibilities for cash flow control and receivables collection are clearly assigned to individuals and business units, linked to financial KPI/KRI indicators and a transparent monitoring mechanism. When risk indicators exceed warning thresholds, the system automatically triggers response actions to bring risks back within acceptable limits, thereby optimizing capital efficiency, protecting shareholder interests, and ensuring legitimate benefits for employees.

### INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY RISK (CYBERSECURITY RISK)

Cybersecurity risk management at VCC is identified as a key pillar in ensuring continuous, safe, and stable business operations in the context of deep digital transformation. This function is implemented in a proactive prevention-oriented approach, combining technical controls, management procedures and user awareness enhancement. The objective is to protect the Corporation’s information systems, data and digital assets against cyberattacks, data leakage, and service disruptions.

VCC builds and operates a centralized cybersecurity monitoring system, integrating key Information Security Risk Indicators (KRIs) that are automatically calculated and continuously tracked. This system enables early detection of abnormal signals such as unauthorized access incidents, security vulnerabilities, access control violations, or system disruptions. When KRIs exceed predefined thresholds, the system automatically activates incident response procedures, requiring relevant units to implement timely corrective measures to minimize impacts and bring risk levels back within acceptable limits.

In parallel with technical solutions, VCC focuses on completing its information security policy and procedural framework, clearly defining the responsibilities of each individual and unit in protecting IT systems. The Corporation also strengthens internal training and communication on cybersecurity awareness. VCC pays special attention to information security risk governance and data protection in accordance with Viettel Group’s security standards. Through the integration of risk management, technology and human factors, VCC is steadily enhancing its cyber defense capabilities, ensuring data security, maintaining brand reputation and supporting sustainable business development.
