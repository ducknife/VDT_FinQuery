# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## III. ASSESSMENT OF THE PERFORMANCE OF THE EXECUTIVE BOARD

### 1. Supervisory Activities

In accordance with the provisions of the Company Charter, Corporate Governance Regulations, internal management rules, and applicable laws, the supervisory activities of the Board of Directors (BOD) include:

- Monitoring the implementation of resolutions and decisions issued by the General Meeting of Shareholders (GMS) and the Board of Directors.
- Supervising and directing the disclosure of information to ensure transparency, completeness, and timeliness in compliance with regulatory requirements.
- Supervising the management and executive operations of the Executive Board to enhance business efficiency, investment effectiveness, and the achievement of approved business targets and plans.

### 2. Board of Directors’ Assessment of the Executive Board

- The Executive Board has demonstrated strong efforts in managing production and business operations, successfully achieving the planned targets and effectively implementing the tasks assigned by the Board of Directors.
- The Executive Board has maintained regular reporting to the Board of Directors on business performance and the progress of implementing resolutions of the General Meeting of Shareholders and the Board of Directors, and has proactively sought the Board’s approval and guidance for matters within its authority.
- The Executive Board has taken decisive actions in managing accounts receivable and inventory, thereby improving operational efficiency and enhancing the Corporation’s capital turnover.
- The Executive Board has been proactive in implementing initiatives related to customer experience, employee experience, partner experience, and corporate culture learning and development.

## IV. PLAN AND STRATEGIC ORIENTATION OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS FOR 2026

### 1. Orientation and Key Tasks for 2026

- Enhance corporate governance efficiency in line with best practices in Asia and ASEAN, striving to improve corporate governance scores and move into the VNGC20 group.
- Accelerate investment in renewable energy, including wind and solar power, and conduct research on potential investments in social housing development.
- Explore and conduct feasibility studies for investment opportunities in the Dominican market.
- Strengthen capabilities in the B2B construction segment to become a contractor for large-scale and key national infrastructure projects.
- Expand market coverage and value in the B2C construction segment, aiming to become the No.1 provider of turnkey housing construction services in the market.
- Capture leadership in the solar energy market, targeting the No.1 position in the residential solar energy solutions segment in Vietnam.

### 2. Key Business and Production Indicators for 2026 (Consolidated)

- Total revenue: VND 15,653 billion, representing an 11% increase compared to 2025.
- Profit after tax: VND 622.4 billion, representing a 3.8% increase compared to 2025.
- Expected dividend payout ratio: 10%–20%.
- Of which, for the Parent Company: total revenue is VND 13,696 billion and profit after tax is VND 596 billion; Return on Equity (ROE) is 26.8%.
