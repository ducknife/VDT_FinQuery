# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## I. PRODUCTION AND BUSINESS SITUATION

### 1. Overview

In 2025, the global political and economic landscape experienced significant complexities. Geopolitical tensions escalated in many regions (Russia–Ukraine, Thailand–Cambodia, U.S.–China–Taiwan, etc.). Trade wars continued to intensify, particularly after new U.S. tariff policies are issued. According to reputable organizations, global economic growth is expected to slow down. The International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) estimate global GDP growth of 3.2% in 2025, while the World Bank (WB) projects a slightly higher rate of 3.3%.

In this context, Vietnam’s economy maintained strong growth with an estimated increase of 8.02% compared to 2024, only lower than the growth rate in 2022 during the 2011–2025 period. The economy size surpassed USD 514 billion, ranking 32nd globally, with GDP per capita of approximately USD 5,026 per year, affirming Vietnam’s position among the fastest-growing economies in the region and worldwide. Notably, Vietnam effectively capitalized on the global shift in investment flows. As of December 31, 2025, total registered foreign direct investment (FDI) reached USD 38.42 billion, up 0.5% year-on-year.

In 2025, Vietnam continued to maintain a stable political environment, providing a solid foundation for economic growth and investment attraction. Reforms aimed at streamlining the political system and administrative restructuring have opened up new development spaces and enhanced growth potential. The Government has intensified economic management efforts toward promoting public investment and developing strategic infrastructure. Total public investment approved by the National Assembly for 2025 reached approximately VND 790 trillion, focusing on large-scale projects such as the North–South Expressway (Phase 2), Long Thanh International Airport, urban ring roads, and logistics infrastructure. At the same time, Vietnam has continued to promote international economic integration through the implementation of new-generation free trade agreements such as CPTPP, EVFTA, and RCEP, thereby expanding export markets and attracting foreign investment inflows.

Resolutions on the revised Electricity Law and the adjusted Power Development Plan VIII have been promulgated, creating significant opportunities for the renewable energy sector. The 2024 Land Law and the amended Real Estate Business Law have established a more transparent legal framework for construction and real estate investment activities. The 2025 Corporate Income Tax Law enables enterprises to further promote investment in science and technology. Especially, the Law on Management and Investment of State Capital in Enterprises No. 68/2025/QH15 opens up new development space and opportunities for enterprises such as VCC.

Alongside these achievements in politics, economy and legal framework, 2025 also recorded unprecedented natural disasters in Vietnam. The East Sea experienced up to 21 storms and tropical depressions—the highest level ever recorded—placing significant pressure on VCC’s business operations, particularly in the Operations & Exploitation (O&E) segment.

In response to these opportunities and challenges, VCC’s leadership promptly implemented strategic adjustments and strengthened governance, ensuring smooth operations and rapid adaptability to market fluctuations. These efforts played a crucial role in enabling VCC to successfully achieve and grow across all key business performance indicators.

In addition, 2025 marked a year in which VCC was entrusted by the Group to participate in several large-scale and key projects, thereby enhancing its capabilities and creating a strong foundation for future growth. During the year, VCC mobilized resources and focused its management efforts to successfully install 6,452 5G stations, making a significant contribution to 22,400 stations of the Group in total. At the same time, VCC has been actively collaborating with other Group entities to implement major infrastructure projects and national key projects.
