# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## III. DEVELOPMENT PLAN / 2026 PLAN

### 1. Business Environment Outlook

Vietnam is setting ambitious expectations with a GDP growth target of at least 10% in 2026, following strong momentum in 2025. This target is supported by four key pillars:

- **Strong growth in public investment**: Public investment capital allocated for 2026 is projected at approximately VND 1.08 quadrillion and is expected to serve as a key driver for Vietnam’s rapid economic development in the coming year.

- **Continued robust growth in import and export activities**: By the end of 2025, total import-export turnover reached USD 930.05 billion, up 18.2% year-on-year, with exports increasing by 17.0% and imports by 19.4%. The Import-Export Department has also set an ambitious target for 2026, aiming at export turnover growth of over 8% compared to 2025.

- **FDI remains a bright spot**: Total registered FDI reached USD 185 billion during the 2021–2025 period, while disbursed FDI in 2025 amounted to USD 27.62 billion—the highest level in five years—reflecting strong confidence from international investors. In 2026, the Government is expected to introduce additional policies to attract foreign investment, such as Golden Visa programs and reductions in land-use costs.

- **Recovery in consumer demand**: After a period of tightened spending following the pandemic, household consumption is expected to rebound strongly from 2026 onward. However, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) projects Vietnam’s growth to slow from 6.5% to 5.6% in 2026 due to anticipated challenges in the global economic environment. In addition, major trading partners such as the United States and China are also expected to face economic headwinds. The World Bank (WB) and the Asian Development Bank (ADB) forecast Vietnam’s growth to moderate to 6.1% and 6.4%, respectively, in 2026.

Although the economy continues to maintain a stable growth rate, 2026 is expected to remain challenging for businesses. Labor shortages—particularly of highly qualified technical personnel—are evident across multiple industries. As of the end of 2024, the proportion of trained workers holding formal degrees or certifications reached only 28.3%. In the construction sector, some companies have introduced aggressive wage policies to attract talent. In addition, natural disasters and extreme weather conditions are expected to remain unpredictable. Meteorological agencies forecast that 2026 may experience fewer storms than average, as the global climate system transitions from La Niña to a neutral phase and potentially toward El Niño. However, this shift may lead to more complicated and less predictable weather patterns, posing risks to various economic sectors.

### 2. Business Directions and Strategic Orientation for 2026

With the 2026 performance theme of “More Proactive, Faster, Bolder, and More Efficient,” the Executive Board has defined business directions for each segment as follows:

- **Operations and maintenance**: Focus on enhancing workforce quality and strengthening discipline in supervision and operational management, ensuring full compliance with KPI commitments to investors.

- **Civil construction**:
  - **B2B**: Strengthen investments in people, processes, tools, and governance to enhance execution capabilities; increase participation in public investment projects alongside traditional real estate projects. Proactively engage in and gradually enter small- and medium-scale industrial construction projects to capture FDI inflows.
  - **B2C**: Continue to reinforce the Company’s leading market position in residential construction coverage; enhance project value through integrated capabilities in design and construction, and a closed-loop ecosystem of solutions for households, including design, construction, equipment supply, energy solutions and maintenance services.

- **Energy & MEP (Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing)**: Focus on capturing the residential solar energy market; strengthen engagement and deployment of large-scale rooftop solar projects. Restructure the organization and enhance technical capabilities in the MEP segment, thereby delivering integrated end-to-end solutions in Construction – Energy – MEP for corporate clients.

- **Infrastructure investment**: Invest in the construction of 1,000 new BTS stations. Accelerate investment in renewable energy, expanding into wind power alongside solar energy. Explore and develop investment plans for social housing projects.
