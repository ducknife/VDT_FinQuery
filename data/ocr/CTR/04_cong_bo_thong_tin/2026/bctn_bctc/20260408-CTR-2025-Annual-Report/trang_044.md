## 4. Key Priorities for 2026

Training and standardization of field technical workforce for Operations & Maintenance:  
Objective: Build a skilled Operations & Maintenance workforce in both quantity and quality, thereby reducing penalties and increasing revenue.

Investment in renewable energy: Objective: Increase the total installed renewable energy capacity invested by VCC to at least 30 MWp.

Enhancing execution capabilities in B2B Construction: Objective: Become a qualified contractor capable of delivering large-scale projects, including high-rise buildings, transport infrastructure, and industrial zone infrastructure; improve execution capabilities to meet the highest domestic standards and align with global trends.

Expanding coverage and value of B2C Construction projects: Objective: Achieve nationwide coverage of residential construction projects across 100% of communes/wards; increase average revenue per project (ARPU) to over VND 1 billion.

Capturing the solar energy market: Objective: Position VCC as a leading player in the market, capturing the residential solar energy segment and expanding VCC solar solutions to 60 new communes/wards in 2026.
