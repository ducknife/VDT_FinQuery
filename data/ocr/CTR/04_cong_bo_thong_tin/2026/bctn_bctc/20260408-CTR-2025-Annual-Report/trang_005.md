# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## CHAIRMAN'S MESSAGE

Dear Shareholders, Customers, Partners and all Employees,

Looking back on 2025 – a year marked by many significant national milestones, including the 95th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of Vietnam; the 80th anniversary of the establishment of the Democratic Republic of Vietnam, now the Socialist Republic of Vietnam; the 50th anniversary of the Liberation of the South and National Reunification; along with Party Congresses at all levels – in contributing to the shared pride of the nation, together with Viettel Group, Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation has made every effort, mobilized maximum resources, and ensured a stable, secure, and high-quality telecommunications network to serve customers during these major national events.

The year 2025 also witnessed unusual natural developments, with increasing intensity and frequency of natural disasters across a wide area. We faced 15 storms, 6 tropical depressions, along with numerous historic floods in many localities. In that context, “Viettel Construction people” demonstrated strong resilience, a high sense of responsibility and silent dedication, as many frontline employees participated in disaster response and recovery efforts for extended periods, far from their families. These efforts not only ensured uninterrupted communications but also reaffirmed the Corporation’s core values and social responsibility.

The year 2025 also holds special significance as it marks the 30th anniversary of Viettel Construction’s establishment and development. Over the past three decades, the Corporation has consistently stood alongside the country through periods of difficulty and challenge, continuously innovating, enhancing its capabilities, and successfully fulfilling its production and business objectives, while fully realizing its mission of “Building a Better Life.”

Entering a new phase of development, the Corporation has formulated its 2026–2030 Strategy with a bold vision and strong aspirations: striving to rank among the Top 100 largest enterprises in Vietnam, while, in each business segment, aiming to achieve market leadership and play a pioneering and leading role.

In parallel with growth objectives, the Corporation continues to enhance its governance system in line with advanced standards, strengthen transparency, risk management, and sustainable development, thereby improving corporate value and maximizing shareholders’ interests.

The achievements over the past 30 years, together with the title of Hero of Labor in the Renewal Period, serve as a solid foundation and strong source of confidence for us to steadfastly implement our strategic objectives in the coming period.

On behalf of the Board of Directors, I would like to express our sincere appreciation to our valued shareholders, customers, partners and all employees for your continued trust, support, and contributions to the Corporation’s development over the years. We are committed to ongoing innovation, enhancing operational efficiency, creating sustainable value, and delivering long-term benefits to all stakeholders.

We look forward to your continued support and partnership on our journey ahead.

Yours Sincerely,

Do Manh Hung  
Chairman of the Board
