# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## CEO'S MESSAGE

### Dear Shareholders,

The year 2025 closed with amid significant fluctuations in both the global and domestic economies. The construction and infrastructure sector has faced increasing competitive pressures, along with higher demands for quality, progress, sustainability, and the application of technology. In this context, Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation has remained steadfast in its long-term development orientation, making comprehensive efforts in innovation to sustain growth momentum and create sustainable value for shareholders.

Looking back on the past year, we are proud that Viettel Construction has continued to maintain a stable growth foundation, expand its operational scale, and gradually affirm its position as a leading technology-driven construction and infrastructure enterprise in Vietnam, with revenue of VND 14,050 billion and profit after tax of VND 745.1 billion, setting a new milestone in its development journey. These achievements are not only the result of the dedication and determination of our employees but also reflect the continued trust, support and companionship of our valued shareholders.

Over the past year, Viettel Construction has further expanded its operations across key business areas. In addition to maintaining its core role in the construction and operation of telecommunications infrastructure, the Corporation has accelerated the development of new business segments such as civil and industrial construction, renewable energy, technical infrastructure operations, and integrated service solutions. These strategic moves not only help diversify revenue streams but also establish a solid foundation for the Corporation’s long-term growth.

One of Viettel Construction’s key strategic directions is comprehensive digital transformation in governance and operations. Over the past year, we have accelerated the application of technology in project management, production operations, quality control, and supply chain optimization. The digitalization of processes not only enhances operational efficiency but also improves transparency, creating a sustainable competitive advantage as the construction industry enters a period of strong transformation.

In parallel with digital transformation, Viettel Construction remains committed to green transition and sustainable development. Clean energy solutions, particularly rooftop solar power and energy-efficient infrastructure models, are increasingly becoming an integral part of the Corporation’s service ecosystem. We firmly believe that economic development must go hand in hand with environmental and social responsibility, which is also an inevitable direction for the future of the construction industry.

Entering 2026 and the years ahead, Viettel Construction aims to further enhance its construction capabilities, expand its market presence, and develop high-tech business segments. We aspire to position Viettel Construction as a comprehensive provider of infrastructure and construction solutions, capable of participating deeply in large-scale domestic projects and gradually expanding into international markets.

Ambitious goals inevitably come with significant challenges. However, with the strong foundation of Viettel’s corporate culture, the pioneering spirit, discipline and creativity of more than 11,000 employees, together with the continued support of our valued shareholders, we are confident that Viettel Construction will sustain its growth momentum, improve operational efficiency, and enhance corporate value in the long term.

On behalf of the Executive Board, I would like to express our sincere gratitude to our valued shareholders for your continued trust and companionship with Viettel Construction over the years. Your support is the driving force that inspires us to continuously innovate, grow and create sustainable value for the Corporation, the community, and society at large.

We wish our valued shareholders good health, success, and continued partnership with Viettel Construction on its journey ahead.

**Yours Sincerely,**

**Pham Dinh Truong**  
Chief Executive Officer
