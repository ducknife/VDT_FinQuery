# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## 3. Legal Compliance Activities

Compliance with applicable laws serves as a fundamental foundation for the Company’s sustainable development. During its operations and implementation of production and business activities, VCC consistently ensures full compliance with prevailing legal regulations and has carried out information disclosure procedures in a transparent and timely manner in accordance with the requirements applicable to a public company.

### Legal compliance activities at VCC include:

**People:** VCC has enhanced the quality of recruitment and specialized its legal personnel. The Company continues to improve the professional competence of its staff through internal training programs and workshops, thereby ensuring effective support in addressing legal issues related to projects and ongoing production and business activities within the Corporation.

**Processes:**
- A legal compliance framework has been developed and implemented to identify applicable legal requirements, thereby enabling timely risk identification and the implementation of appropriate preventive and responsive measures.
- The Company proactively monitors and updates changes in legal regulations, reviews internal governance documents and regulations and develops appropriate procedures to strengthen compliance across the Corporation.
- The Company has contributed comments to draft legislation closely related to its business operations across key sectors, including telecommunications, data, investment, state capital management and investment in enterprises, construction, and taxation (15 legal documents in 2025).

**Tools:** Legal compliance activities are further supported through digital transformation initiatives, including the development of tools and software systems to enhance management efficiency and ensure smooth operational continuity across the Corporation.

**Governance:** Periodic post-audit legal compliance reviews are conducted to assess effectiveness and require corrective actions for identified issues within relevant units.

## 4. Ethics and Conduct

- VCC has issued and implemented a Code of Conduct comprising eight key principles: (1) Conduct in the workplace; (2) Conduct in job performance; (3) Conduct with colleagues; (4) Conduct with customers; (5) Conduct with partners; (6) Conduct with peer enterprises; (7) Conduct with the community; and (8) Conduct in cyberspace.
- The Corporation’s Code of Conduct serves as a practical framework that enables the maximization of individual capabilities, enhances work efficiency, and fosters a professional working style along with civilized and standard behaviors in alignment with corporate culture.
- The Corporation expects the Code of Conduct to serve not only as a set of regulations but also as a guiding principle accompanying all employees, being understood and naturally applied in daily work and communication. Compliance with these ethical standards not only strengthens internal cohesion but also contributes to building a professional image and enhancing VCC’s reputation among customers, partners, and the community.
- The Code of Conduct provides specific guidelines on expected behavior for employees, including members of the Board of Directors and Executive Board in all interactions with relevant stakeholders.
- The Board of Directors is responsible for overseeing the implementation of the Code of Conduct and defining the roles of the Executive Board and middle management in executing and monitoring compliance with the Code.
- VCC has established channels, focal points, and procedures for receiving feedback, complaints, and reporting violations.
