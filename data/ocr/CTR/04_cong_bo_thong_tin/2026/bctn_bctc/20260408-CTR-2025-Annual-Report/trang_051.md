# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## II. BOARD OF DIRECTORS’S ASSESSMENT ON RESPONSIBILITIES TO STAKEHOLDERS

Viettel Construction, in its journey from a well-performing enterprise toward sustainable development, has formulated its growth strategy in alignment with corporate social responsibility under the 3P model: People, Planet, and Profit.

### 1. For Society/Community:

- The Corporation is committed to making the greatest possible contribution to the community. It actively participates in poverty alleviation programs, support for schools, and assistance for disadvantaged individuals.
- The Corporation has carried out various social and charitable activities with tangible results, including total contributions of VND 106 million to the Child Protection Fund; VND 244 million to the Trade Union “Warm Shelter and Comradeship” Fund; and VND 2.821 billion to the “For the Poor” Fund. In addition, internal engagement activities have been implemented across the Corporation in accordance with the principle that each trade union unit organizes at least one sports, cultural, or artistic exchange activity per fund, with the grassroots trade union supporting 100% of its subordinate units, totaling nearly VND 500 million per fund.
- With the business philosophy of “developing production and business activities in parallel with social and community responsibility,” the Corporation has consistently maintained monthly activities of caring for, visiting, and paying tribute to fallen heroes at the Martyrs’ Cemetery in Me Tri Ward (Nam Tu Liem, Hanoi) on the first day of the lunar month, as well as at martyrs’ cemeteries across all 34 provinces and cities nationwide. Furthermore, the Corporation actively participates in social welfare and people-to-people diplomacy activities, including donations of VND 579 million in support of the people of Cuba; assistance for the construction of 10 houses for policy beneficiaries and disadvantaged households in Quang Ngai Province with total funding of VND 800 million; and a contribution of VND 50 million to support tree planting activities in Truong Sa (Spratly Islands).

### 2. For the Environment: Our objectives are as follows:

- To ensure zero violations of environmental-related legal requirements.
- To enhance the use of renewable or reusable energy sources, promote energy efficiency, reduce greenhouse gas emissions, and ensure that at least one environmental initiative/practice is implemented and applied in practice.

### 3. For employees:

- VCC is a shared home and a learning and career development environment. The Corporation’s viewpoint is to establish a “learning organization culture” with a philosophy of “proactive learning,” whereby each individual defines their own learning pathway to effectively perform their job. In 2025, the Corporation focused on developing and successfully deploying the CALS self-directed learning platform. A total of 1,383 training courses were delivered during the year, with thousands of e-learning lectures conducted, aligning with modern training trends in the new era.
- The average annual training time per employee is 83.64 hours.
- Training programs aimed at enhancing employee awareness and skills in 2025 were focused on four main areas: core value and compliance framework training; advanced skills, professional and technical training; management and leadership development; and training aligned with strategic transformation and business plan implementation.
- The percentage of employees who undergo periodic performance evaluation and career development assessments: In 2025, a total of 141,463 training participations were recorded for employees in skills development and work efficiency enhancement. These activities have delivered positive outcomes for both individuals and the organization, contributing to improved labor productivity and the successful completion of the Corporation’s 2025 production and business plan.
- VCC consistently recognizes that the value created by each individual contributes to the overall success of the enterprise. Without discrimination based on gender, age, ethnicity, or vulnerable social groups, VCC fosters a dynamic, transparent, fair, and inclusive working environment.
- The Corporation identifies Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) as a core element of its sustainable development strategy, aiming to protect employees’ rights, ensure operational stability in production and business activities, and support long-term growth. The Corporation has developed a structured OSH implementation plan with a clear roadmap and has signed agreements with specialized partners (the Occupational Health Center – Institute of Occupational Safety and Hygiene Science) to implement key activities such as workplace environmental monitoring, identification of jobs with occupational disease risks, and defining job categories requiring occupational health examinations in the organization.
- The Corporation consistently pays close attention to employee welfare and maintains a strong and constructive relationship between management and employees, fostering mutual understanding, a shared mindset, and aligned working practices, while promoting the “Uncle Ho’s Soldier” tradition.
- In terms of social mobilization and employee welfare policies, the Corporation regularly cares for and supports social organizations and the living conditions of its employees. A notable initiative is the implementation of the special policy program “Solidarity Houses.” In 2025, the Corporation approved the construction of 31 “Solidarity Houses.” In addition, the policy of “VND 20 million support for VCC couples upon marriage” continues to receive strong engagement across the Corporation. In 2025, 04 couples were awarded this support, with a total funding of VND 80 million.
- The care and encouragement of employees’ children have been implemented in a coordinated and effective manner. The Corporation organized Mid-Autumn Festival activities for 13,461 children nationwide, with a total expenditure of VND 4.038 billion; and awarded scholarships to 4,944 students with excellent academic performance in the 2025–2026 school year, with total funding of VND 1.288 billion. During Workers’ Month 2025, the Corporation gave financial assistance to 117 employees and their families facing difficulties, with a total support amount of VND 752 million; and organized visits and gift-giving activities on the occasion of War Invalids and Martyrs Day (27 July) for 595 relatives, with a total value of VND 308 million. In response to the impacts of natural disasters and floods, the Corporation promptly provided support to 470 employees and their families, with total assistance amounting to VND 3.348 billion. In addition, Tet (Lunar New Year) care activities were carried out comprehensively, with total expenditure of over VND 1 billion, including gifts presented to 81 retired employees, 15 families of martyrs, families of employees and relatives who passed away in 2025, 21 employees suffering from serious or long-term illnesses, and 123 relatives of employees.

### 4. For suppliers:

- Procurement practices: With an operating model in 34 provinces and cities nationwide, VCC has established dedicated organizational units at provincial level with capabilities in procurement and contractor selection to meet the demand for goods and services in order to serve production and business activities. As a result, thousands of contracts have been signed with numerous local suppliers and partners. The proportion of local procurement reaches approximately 50% of the total procurement value of VCC, which is also reflected in the Corporation’s overall revenue structure.
- Procurement process: In the supplier selection and evaluation process, VCC does not rely solely on traditional criteria such as financial capacity, production capability and after-sales service. The Corporation is committed to ensuring that supplier evaluation and selection also incorporate environmental and social criteria to support long-term sustainable development, in line with VCC’s strategic orientation.
