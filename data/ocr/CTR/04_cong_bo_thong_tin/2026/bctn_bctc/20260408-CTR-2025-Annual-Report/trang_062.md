# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## Supervision of implementation of the General Meeting of Shareholders’ Resolutions

In 2025, the global economic and political environment continued to experience significant volatility, prolonged risks and uncertainties. Geopolitical tensions in several hotspots such as Russia–Ukraine, Iran–Israel, and Thailand–Cambodia have shown no signs of easing, exerting pressure on global supply chains, capital flows and market sentiment. At the same time, the impacts of climate change have become increasingly evident, leading to a higher frequency and severity of extreme weather events, which are becoming more intense, irregular and concentrated. Vietnam remains among the countries most severely affected, with 12 storms accompanied by floods causing serious damage in many regions nationwide.

Domestically, the Government and ministries have been strongly promoting digital transformation, institutional reform and the improvement of the legal and policy framework, thereby expanding the legal corridor and creating growth momentum. The two-tier local government model has initially operated stably and effectively. Major national commemorative events have been successfully organized, contributing to enhancing Vietnam’s image, capacity, position, and international prestige.

Vietnam’s economy in 2025 maintained a positive recovery momentum, with GDP growth reaching 8.02% and average CPI increasing by 3.31%, remaining within the controlled target range. The trade balance continued to record a surplus, and FDI inflows remained stable, thereby supporting production expansion and domestic consumption growth.

In the context of an unpredictable market environment and emerging risks, the Board of Directors and Executive Board proactively monitored developments, applied flexible management, and implemented synchronized governance solutions to ensure stable operations and the achievement of business targets assigned by the General Meeting of Shareholders. At the same time, capital preservation and development remained a priority through effective cash flow management, optimization of resource utilization, strengthened financial risk governance, and strict compliance with legal regulations.

## Supervising the Board of Directors

In 2025, the Supervisory Board fully performed its supervisory functions over the activities of the Board of Directors and the Executive Board, ensuring independence, objectivity and compliance with applicable laws, the Company’s Charter and resolutions of the General Meeting of Shareholders. The Supervisory Board attended all meetings of the Board of Directors during the year, monitored the preparation of agendas and meeting materials, the issuance of resolutions and supervised compliance in the implementation process.

In 2025, the Board of Directors held 11 meetings (including 04 voting meetings and 07 written consultation sessions), ensuring compliance with procedures and regulations. Meeting agendas were carefully prepared and circulated to members in advance as required. Members actively participated in discussions and provided professional input, thereby clearly demonstrating the Board’s role in directing, planning and supervising governance activities.

The Board of Directors fully exercised its authority in accordance with regulations, focusing on key matters such as approval of the 2025 business plan, settlement of the 2024 salary fund, orientation and review of investment-related matters during the period, and other important governance issues. All matters were discussed and considered in alignment with the Corporation’s development strategy and actual operational requirements. The Supervisory Board closely monitored the legality, validity of documentation, authorization authority and the appropriateness of the resolutions and decisions issued.

In addition, the Board of Directors issued and updated several internal regulations, adjusted the organizational structure and strengthened management personnel in line with practical requirements and the Company’s development orientation. The Supervisory Board monitored these activities to ensure legal compliance, proper authority, transparency and alignment with the governance structure of the Corporation.

The organization of the 2025 Annual General Meeting of Shareholders was directed by the Board of Directors in compliance with applicable regulations, ensuring full adherence to procedures, content requirements, documentation and information disclosure in accordance with the Law on Enterprises and the Law on Securities. The Supervisory Board monitored the preparation and conduct of the AGM, thereby noting the strict compliance with requirements on disclosure, transparency, and shareholders’ rights.

## Supervising the Executive Board/Board of Management

With regard to the Executive Board, the Supervisory Board monitored the implementation of resolutions of the General Meeting of Shareholders and the Board of Directors, as well as the level of compliance and performance against assigned targets. In 2025, business and production activities were implemented in a coordinated manner under the approved plan, ensuring progress of key tasks. Operational activities were managed with a focus on improving capital efficiency, increasing revenue and profit growth while maintaining financial safety in the context of continued market volatility. The Executive Board also proactively expanded markets and promoted the development of new business areas beyond the traditional core sectors, thereby diversifying revenue sources and enhancing long-term competitiveness. The Supervisory Board highly appreciates the proactive, flexible approach and the continuous efforts in innovation in management and governance.

## Coordination between the Supervisory Board, the Board of Directors, the Executive Board and other management personnel

In 2025, the Supervisory Board maintained the principles of independence and objectivity in performing its duties while strengthening coordination with the Board of Directors, the Executive Board and other management personnel to enhance supervisory effectiveness and governance quality. The Supervisory Board attended all meetings of the Board of Directors, was provided with relevant documents in accordance with regulations and proactively expressed opinions and recommendations when necessary.

Coordination with the Executive Board was carried out on the basis of timely, transparent information exchange and data sharing to support evaluation, supervision and risk monitoring and early warning activities. In working with functional management personnel, the Supervisory Board accessed actual operational information to conduct in-depth inspection and review while providing appropriate improvement recommendations to enhance governance effectiveness and internal control. Coordination activities were implemented in a unified and synchronized manner across departments, thereby contributing to ensuring that the Corporation operates in line with its strategic direction, complies with applicable laws and improves overall governance efficiency.

## Other activities of the Supervisory Board

In addition to periodic supervision under the work plan, in 2025 the Supervisory Board carried out a number of professional activities to enhance supervisory capacity, support the governance system and strengthen internal control effectiveness. Members of the Supervisory Board participated in training and development programs on internal audit, corporate governance, securities law and supervisory skills. At the same time, the Supervisory Board conducted on-site inspections at units to assess project implementation, receivables management and internal control practices. Based on these activities, the Board promptly identified issues and made appropriate recommendations for corrective actions.

The above activities contributed effectively to the supervisory function, enhancing the proactiveness and execution capacity of the Supervisory Board, thereby supporting the strengthening of governance discipline, improving transparency and enhancing the overall governance efficiency of the Corporation in 2025.
