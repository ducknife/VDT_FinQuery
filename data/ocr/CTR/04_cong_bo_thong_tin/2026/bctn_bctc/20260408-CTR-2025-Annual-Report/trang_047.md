# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## V. ASSESSMENT OF THE REPORT ON THE COMPANY'S ENVIRONMENTAL AND SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITIES

### 1. Environmental Performance Assessment

#### ① Waste Management

Environmental protection is one of the key factors enabling sustainable business development. VCC applies ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 14064-1:2018 standards to identify significant environmental aspects, as well as to monitor and inventory greenhouse gas emissions.

**Key activities at VCC:**
1. Waste segregation and collection at source.
2. Engagement of qualified partners for waste transportation.
3. Application of ISO 14001:2015 across offices, warehouses, and construction projects to ensure significant environmental aspects are effectively controlled with timely response measures.

**Significant environmental aspects identified at VCC:**
1. Discharge of domestic wastewater from employees’ daily activities.
2. Gas leakage and fire/explosion risks during gas usage for cooking.
3. CO₂ emissions from electricity and fuel (gasoline/diesel) consumption.
4. Dust and noise generation from movement and operations at construction sites/projects.
5. Generation of waste oil from the use of construction equipment and vehicles.
6. Fire, explosion, and electric shock risks arising from employees’ daily activities.

**For the significant environmental aspects identified, VCC has taken the following measures:**
1. Waste collection at source.
2. Selection of qualified partners for waste transportation and disposal.
3. Development of guidelines for efficient use of electricity and water.
4. Periodic maintenance of vehicles.
5. Establishment of criteria for leasing construction equipment and machinery.
6. Monitoring and measurement of workplace environmental conditions.
7. Avoid simultaneous operation of multiple high-noise equipment/machinery in densely populated areas.
8. Water spraying at construction sites to control dust.
9. Installation of protective barriers around construction areas.
10. Application of ISO 45001 to ensure occupational health and safety, including development of emergency response plans (fire/explosion, electric shock, chemical spills) and organization of annual drills.

**Environmental performance in 2025:**
1. No incidents of chemical spills occurred at construction sites.
2. 100% of transportation vehicles were maintained on a periodic basis.
3. No incidents of fire, explosion, or occupational accidents related to electric shock or gas usage were recorded.
4. All domestic waste generated from office operations and project construction activities was collected in covered, designated bins and transported to the building’s storage area or the Investor’s designated waste collection area.
5. All hazardous waste (e.g., lightbulbs, printer cartridges, paint containers) was properly segregated into designated containers with labeling and identification prior to being transferred to the building’s or Investor’s storage facilities.
6. All domestic wastewater at construction sites was properly collected and directed through drainage systems into centralized collection tanks connected to the Investor’s wastewater treatment system.
7. VCC implemented single-sided printed paper reuse initiatives in its operations to support core products and services.

#### ② Greenhouse Gas Emissions Management

In its efforts to mitigate greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, VCC plans to establish a system for controlling emission sources and conducting GHG inventories in accordance with ISO 14064-1:2018 from 2024. Key initiatives for GHG mitigation and environmental protection at VCC include:

1. Effective management of emission sources and waste.
2. Promotion of green energy.
3. Investment in environmentally friendly technologies.
4. Enhancement of innovation initiatives related to GHG emission reduction.
5. Promotion of resource-saving practices, including efficient use of electricity, water, and paper.

### 2. Assessment of Labor-Related Issues

VCC recognizes that the right people are a critical asset of the organization. “People” are considered the foremost factor among the four key drivers (people – process – technology – governance) in addressing business challenges at VCC. Accordingly, all VCC policies are developed based on a comprehensive assessment of their impact on employees, ensuring the protection of their legitimate rights and interests. The Company is committed to building and maintaining a working environment where all employees feel valued, respected, and empowered to fully realize their potential and contribute to the Company’s overall development. In 2025, VCC continued to be recognized among the Top 100 Best Places to Work in Vietnam and ranked No.1 in the Construction/Architecture sector according to a survey conducted by Anphabe.
