# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## 5. Risk Management Activities in 2025

### PEOPLE

At VCC, Risk Management Culture is identified as one of the six core cultural pillars, playing a foundational role in safeguarding the Corporation against increasingly complicated market fluctuations. Risk management is not merely a management function but has been shaped into a behavioral standard, deeply embedded in the mindset and daily working practices of employees at all levels.

On a quarterly basis, business centers proactively integrate risk management content into professional training programs for frontline staff, thereby ensuring that each employee clearly understands their role and responsibility in identifying, controlling and preventing risks. In parallel, centers and departments maintain regular communication activities on risk management through the “Companion” channel, with a total of 60 articles published during the year, contributing to raising compliance awareness, improving vertical operational quality and strengthening risk prevention capabilities across the system.

The Corporation also promotes an open and transparent communication mechanism through risk reporting channels, encouraging employees to proactively report and give early warnings of potential risks. At the same time, the development of risk and fraud scenarios, along with corresponding response plans, is systematically implemented at multiple levels, thereby enabling the organization to be well-prepared to respond to uncertainties.

In 2025, VCC’s Risk Management Community organized 108 professional workshops, through which 391 risks were identified and 514 mitigation measures were proposed across three levels. Numerous risk management initiatives were discussed, refined, and applied in practice; among them, 36 outstanding initiatives were recognized and rewarded, contributing to fostering innovation, enhancing risk control capabilities, and strengthening the foundation for the Corporation’s sustainable development.

### PROCESS

100% of business plans, investment projects and critical operational processes are subject to risk identification and the integration of control checkpoints from the design stage, ensuring that risks are managed proactively rather than through formalistic controls or post-event remediation.

To ensure timeliness, consistency and accuracy in governance, the Corporation has refined and completed the Risk Management Maturity Assessment framework for 52 units in alignment with the new organizational model. In parallel, a maturity assessment framework at the corporate level has been developed, consistent with the 2025 governance objectives and aligned with the COSO risk management framework.

All 52 risk profiles across the Corporation are periodically reviewed and updated to reflect changes in the business environment, legal framework, technology, and market conditions. At the same time, the Corporation continues to develop and refine the processes and regulations as a unified legal framework, facilitating effective implementation across units. It also strengthens review mechanisms, inspection processes, and cross-unit control coordination to enhance governance effectiveness and optimize resource utilization across the entire system.
