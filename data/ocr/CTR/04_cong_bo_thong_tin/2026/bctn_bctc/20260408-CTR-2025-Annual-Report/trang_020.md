# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## COMPLIANCE RISK

Non-compliance risk management at VCC is implemented in a proactive and systematic manner to ensure that all business activities, investments, and operations fully comply with applicable laws, regulations, charters, and internal rules, thereby minimizing legal, financial, and reputational risks. Compliance activities are integrated throughout the entire value chain—from strategic planning, policy development, and contract signing to project execution and operational management—with a strong emphasis on preventing risks from the outset.

On this basis, VCC has established a unified compliance framework across the Corporation, clearly identifying key legal obligations, non-compliance risks, and corresponding control measures for each business area, function, and job position. In addition, the Corporation implements a compliance scoring mechanism for individuals and units based on specific and objective criteria, linked to performance results, violation levels, implementation of corrective actions, and the degree of proactiveness in identifying and reporting risks.

Compliance risk indicators (KRIs) and compliance scores are automatically monitored and calculated on the management system, enabling early detection of violations or declining compliance levels. When indicators exceed predefined thresholds, the system automatically triggers requirements for review, corrective actions and preventive measures to bring risks back within acceptable limits.

In parallel, the Corporation strengthens training and capacity building on legal knowledge and internal procedures for management personnel. It also enhances post-audit activities, as well as periodic and ad-hoc inspections across units to promptly detect violations, address governance gaps, and prevent recurrence. Through these integrated measures, VCC is progressively reinforcing regulatory discipline, enhancing a culture of compliance and building sustainable trust with customers and shareholders.

## REPUTATIONAL RISK

Reputational risk at VCC may arise from any negative feedback in the digital environment, customer dissatisfaction, unfavorable media or press coverage, as well as delays or insufficiencies in official communications. These factors can directly impact the Corporation’s image, reputation, and brand value in the market. Recognizing the fast-spreading nature and wide-ranging impact of this type of risk, VCC identifies reputational risk management as a key pillar within its enterprise risk management framework.

To proactively control this risk, VCC has developed and operates a customer care system that shifts from a reactive model to a proactive management approach through the application of technology and artificial intelligence (AI). The system automatically collects, measures, and analyzes customer feedback data, monitors reputational KRIs, forecasts behavioral trends, and detects early warning signals. This enables relevant units to promptly engage and handle feedback, thereby improving customer experience.

In parallel, the Corporation maintains a transparent and proactive information disclosure mechanism through investor conferences, official communication channels and timely reporting practices. Ensuring that shareholders, partners, and customers have access to accurate, complete and consistent information helps reduce misinformation risks, strengthen market confidence, and safeguard VCC’s long-term brand reputation.
