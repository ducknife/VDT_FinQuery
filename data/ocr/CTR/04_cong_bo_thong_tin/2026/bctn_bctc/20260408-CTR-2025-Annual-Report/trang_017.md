# VI. DEVELOPMENT ORIENTATION

## VISION
To become the leading investor, operator, and provider of smart and highly efficient infrastructure.

## MISSION
To promote digital transformation of infrastructure and smart facilities for enterprises and households; provide professional technical services with high quality and competitive cost.

## 2030 Objectives
- Ranked among the Top 100 largest revenue enterprises in Vietnam (VNR500).
- Providing a fully integrated ecosystem of Consulting – Design – Implementation – Operation – Optimization – Investment, achieving Top 1–3 market positions across key business segments, including Operations & Maintenance (O&M), Infrastructure Investment, Construction, and Technical Solutions & Services.

## Specific objectives
- Total revenue is expected to reach VND 28,790 billion by 2030, with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 15.6% over the period.
- Profit before tax is expected to reach VND 1,315 billion by 2030, with a pre-tax profit margin of 4.6%, and a CAGR of 12% per year over the period.
- Business solutions:
  - Expanding business into new products and services, including: (i) Technical infrastructure: IoT infrastructure, EV charging stations, data centers, traffic cameras, etc.; (ii) Investment in industrial park infrastructure and renewable energy projects; (iii) Construction: real estate business, interiors, EPC smart city projects, etc.; (iv) Technical solutions and services: solar energy projects in overseas markets.
  - Proactively researching and seizing opportunities to participate in large-scale, key national projects.
  - Expanding business into new markets such as the Dominican Republic and Australia; proactively exploring and identifying opportunities in markets and representative offices where the Group has investment orientations.
