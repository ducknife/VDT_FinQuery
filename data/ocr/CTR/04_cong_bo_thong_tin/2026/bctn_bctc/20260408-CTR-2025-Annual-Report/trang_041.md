# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## 2. Implementation of Corporate Experience Initiatives

### ① Customer Experience

> **Objectives and orientation**: To lead and orient Customer Experience activities across the entire Corporation in accordance with 84 criteria of the TM Forum, covering six pillars: Customer, Strategy, Technology, Operations, Culture and Data, targeting a maturity level of 4.85/5 by 2026; and become a leading unit that promotes awareness, understanding and implementation of Customer Experience enhancement practices across the entire partner ecosystem.

> **Specific target metrics**:
> - Challenge level: Customer perception metrics, including CES, CSAT and NPS across all business areas, are targeted to reach 99%. Outcome metrics such as repeat purchases, contract renewals, customer referrals and cross-selling of products and services are targeted at 50%.
> - Expected level: Both perception and outcome metrics are expected to outperform 2025 results and exceed industry benchmarks, with a minimum growth of 30%. All 34 Viettel provincial/city branches are expected to implement customer referral programs with repeat purchase and renewal rates of 50%. Detailed KPIs are defined under the Customer Experience KPI framework issued for 2026 for each specific indicator.

> **Processes and regulations**: The entire Corporation and its units comply with and implement the following processes/regulations:
> - Service quality measurement and survey process
> - Customer feedback/complaint handling process
> - Operational action plan to improve Customer Experience maturity score in 2026

> **Achievements**:
> - Awards: In January 2026, Viettel Construction was honored at the Stevie Awards for Sales and Customer Service 2026, one of the world's most prestigious award systems in the fields of sales and customer experience. This achievement not only recognizes outstanding service quality but also further affirms Viettel Construction's position on the global customer service map.
> - Customer Experience maturity level: 2024 (2.52 points), 2025 (4.35 points) — an increase of 1.83 points. VCC's Customer Experience has transitioned into the enhancement stage, moving beyond the initial awareness stage in 2024.
> - Outcome indicators: Customer referral/repeat purchase rates: All 3/3 business segments achieved KPI targets: Technical Services (37%/36%); Integrated Solutions (3.7%/2%); Construction B2C (3.5%/2%). Number of referred customers: 61 Construction B2C customers (~3.5% of customers signing contracts in 2025), corresponding revenue of approximately VND 61.2 billion; 168 solar energy customers (~3.7% of customers signing contracts in 2025), corresponding revenue of approximately VND 17 billion. Number of repeat technical service orders: 17,470 orders (~37% of total orders in 2025).
> - Perception metrics (Customer Satisfaction – CSAT; Customer Effort Score – CES; Net Promoter Score – NPS); all exceeded industry benchmarks and recorded growth compared to 2024.

> **Roadmap**: In 2026, Customer Experience management maturity is targeted to reach Level 4.85 (an increase of 0.5 points compared to 2025).

### ② Employee Experience

> **Objectives and orientation**: To lead and align Employee Experience (EX) initiatives across the entire Corporation in accordance with 150 criteria of TM Forum, covering 13 pillars, targeting a maturity level of 4.30/5 by 2026. Entering 2026, the concept of human resource management globally has undergone significant transformation, moving beyond traditional values toward an era of digitalized and personalized employee experience. In the context where AI technologies are deeply embedded across operational processes, building an ideal workplace is no longer limited to basic benefits but focuses on creating “empathetic touchpoints” driven by data. Current global trends emphasize continuous listening capabilities through real-time analytics systems, enabling organizations not only to understand but also to anticipate and respond promptly to employees’ needs and expectations. In this context, 2026 is defined as the year of “Elevating governance and synchronizing experience.” The Corporation will transition from a campaign-based implementation mindset to a data-driven management approach, positioning Employee Experience as a continuous, integrated flow, eliminating gaps between headquarters and branches and ensuring that every employee—regardless of position—receives optimal care and support from the organization.

> **Specific target indicators**:
> - In 2026, the Employee Experience maturity level of the Corporation is targeted to reach 4.3/5 (an increase of 0.3 points compared to 2025).
> - Candidate experience score is targeted at 4.8/5 (an increase of 0.1 points compared to 2025).
> - Employee experience score is targeted at 4.0/5 (an increase of 0.4 points compared to 2025).
> - eNPS (Employee Net Promoter Score) is targeted at 80% (an increase of 23% compared to 2025).
> - ESI (Employee Satisfaction Index) is targeted at 85% (an increase of 5% compared to 2025).

> **Processes and regulations**:
> - Standardize the onboarding process on the first working day to demonstrate “Respect” and professionalism.
> - Issue regulations and processes for performance management (PMS), ensuring fairness and transparency in evaluation.

> **Achievements**:
> - Employee Experience maturity level: 2025 (4.0 points)
> - Outcome metrics:
>   » Labor productivity: Revenue-based labor productivity in 2025 reached VND 1,159 million per employee, achieving 100.4% of the plan (target: VND 1,154 million per employee), representing a 20% increase compared to 2024 (actual 2024: VND 965 million per employee).
>   » Attrition rate: Total employee turnover in 2025 was 1,125 employees (equivalent to 11%), a decrease of 260 employees compared to 2024 (down 2% versus 2024), achieving 100% of the plan.
>   » Innovation initiative participation rate (SKYT): A total of 4,012 initiatives were registered in 2025 (accounting for 38% of total employees), an increase of 716 initiatives compared to 2024 (up 9%), achieving 107% of the plan.

> **Roadmap**: In 2026, Employee Experience management maturity is targeted to reach Level 4.3 (an increase of 0.3 points compared to 2025).
