## 2. Assessment of business and production performance

### ① Achievements

- Successfully completed the 2025 production and business targets, achieving double-digit growth and ensuring stable employee income despite a highly volatile and unpredictable market environment.
- Maintained the No.1 position in the Vietnam TowerCo market with a cumulative total of 12,000 towers as of the end of 2025.
- Played a core role in Viettel’s 5G rollout, completing the installation of 6,452 5G stations, contributing significantly to 22,400 stations of the Group in total in the year.
- Continued shifting the revenue structure toward new business segments and external customers. All new business areas achieved double-digit growth compared to 2024. Revenue from external customers increased by 15% year-on-year, raising its share from 39.7% in 2024 to 41% in 2025.
- Digital transformation activities continued to be strongly implemented, reaching a digital transformation index of 4.16/5, with all 6 business areas achieving Level 4 or above (Advanced level).

### ② Risks and areas for improvement

- The construction segment did not meet planned targets due to a decline in project workload from public investment during the transition period of the two-tier local government model and administrative restructuring (including provincial and agency mergers). Many construction projects were delayed or stalled due to issues related to permitting procedures, site handover, etc. leading to prolonged implementation timelines.
- The M&E (Mechanical & Electrical) segment recorded lower-than-expected growth. The main reason was the weak recovery in retail consumer demand, particularly for non-essential products. In addition, M&E projects funded by public investment were also negatively impacted by the same factors affecting the construction segment.
