### Detailed Description

**Board of Directors:** 5 members, including 1 independent member

**Supervisory Board:** 3 members

**Executive Board/Board of Management:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and 4 Deputy CEOs.

**Business Support Division:**
- Corporate Departments (10 departments): Political Affairs; Human Resources; Administration Office; Finance & Accounting; Procurement; Branding & Communications; Legal & Internal Control; Engineering & Technology; Business Strategy; Customer Care
- Information Technology Center

**Business Division (5 centers):**
- O & E sector: Operations and Exploitation Center
- Solutions & technical services sector: Solutions and Technical Services Center
- Telecommunications construction & infrastructure investment sector: Infrastructure Investment Center
- Civil construction sector: B2C Construction Center; B2B Construction Center

**Direct Production & Business Division:**
- 34 Viettel Provincial/City Construction Branches
- Overseas market companies: 2 companies operating in Myanmar and Cambodia
