# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## 2. Sub-committees under the Board of Director

The Board of Directors currently has two sub-committees: the Secretariat of BODs and the Internal Audit Committee (established on the basis of restructuring the Internal Audit Department).

### ① Board Secretariat Committee:

In addition to organizing General Meetings of Shareholders (GMS) and Board of Directors (BOD) meetings, the Board Secretariat Committee has supported BOD members in conducting supervisory thematic reviews, as well as activities related to reviewing legal regulations, updating and amending internal governance documents, and researching and assessing corporate governance models in line with best practices. In addition, while ensuring compliance with applicable regulations, the Board Secretariat Committee also performs the following tasks:

- **Information disclosure activities**: During the year, the Corporation disclosed 21 items of information, ensuring compliance with regulations as well as providing complete, accurate, and timely information.

- **IR activities**:
  - The Corporation has participated in numerous meetings with investors and major investment funds at various events (such as the HSC’s Emerging Vietnam Conference 2025) as well as direct meetings at the Corporation. Approximately 40–60 investment funds have had opportunities to meet with the Corporation’s leadership to obtain updates on business operations. The IR department also maintains an online channel to receive and respond to shareholders’ inquiries.
  - On a monthly basis, the Corporation regularly discloses business results and operational updates.

### ② Internal Audit Committee

On April 29, 2025, the Board of Directors of the Corporation made a decision to establish the Internal Audit Committee under the Board of Directors based on the Internal Audit Department.

The internal audit activities conducted in 2025 are as follows:

- **Audit of one Business Center**: Assessment of operational efficiency; review of compliance with processes and regulations in project sourcing and project engagement; contractor selection; construction implementation, construction methods, completion and settlement of works; assessment of debt management and recovery; evaluation of subcontractor capacity, project progress, settlement and contract liquidation; and issuance of key recommendations.

- **Audit of one provincial/city branch**: Focus on business performance, business sectors, organizational model, governance, financial and asset management, warehouse management; labor organization, training and communication; material settlement and infrastructure investment; compliance with regulations, internal rules of the Corporation and legal requirements; and issuance of key recommendations.

- **Audit of accounts receivable management at the Corporation**: Evaluation of the adequacy of control design and the level of compliance in the operation of control points within processes and regulations related to receivables management and monitoring; along with key recommendations.

- **Audit of planning, implementation, and management of sales channel development activities at the Corporation**: Assessment of sales channel plan completion, level of compliance with processes and regulations related to planning, implementation, and management of sales channel development activities at the Corporation.

> The Internal Audit function completed the 2025 internal audit plan using a risk-based approach, focusing on activities and processes with high risk levels or significant impact on the Corporation’s business objectives.

> In addition to audit assignments, the Internal Audit Committee completed the review and refinement of the maturity assessment framework and the roadmap to enhance internal audit maturity toward 2030. It also coordinated with an independent consultant to review internal audit activities at the Corporation as a basis for improving and standardizing the system of processes, regulations, and operations in line with international best practices.

> During its operations, the Internal Audit Committee has fully complied with the Internal Audit Regulations approved by the Board of Directors, as well as relevant regulations issued by the Government and the Ministry of Finance, Vietnamese Internal Audit Standards, professional ethical principles, and other applicable regulations.
