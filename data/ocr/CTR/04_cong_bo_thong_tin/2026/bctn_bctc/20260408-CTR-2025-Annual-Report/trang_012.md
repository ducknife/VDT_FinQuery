## II. ESTABLISHMENT AND DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation was established on October 30, 1995 as an independent accounting entity and an integral part of the ecosystem of the Military Industry and Telecommunications Group (Viettel Group). With a workforce of more than 11,000 employees and an operating network in 34 provinces and cities across Vietnam, Viettel Construction is currently a leading technical service provider in Vietnam. The Corporation operates across four core business areas: Infrastructure Investment; Construction (including telecommunications infrastructure construction and B2C & B2B civil construction); Technical Solutions & Services; and Operations & Maintenance.

After 30 years of operation, the Corporation has built a domestic telecommunications infrastructure system comprising more than 50,000 base transceiver stations and 320,000 km of fiber optic cable (equivalent to 8 times the Earth's circumference), covering 100% of Vietnam's territory, including islands, coastal areas, and remote regions. In addition to the domestic market, Viettel Construction has also participated in telecommunications infrastructure development in 10 countries across Asia, Africa, and the Americas.
