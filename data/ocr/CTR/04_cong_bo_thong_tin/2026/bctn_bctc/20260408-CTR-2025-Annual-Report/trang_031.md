# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## 3. Financial Analysis

### BUSINESS PERFORMANCE RESULTS

- Revenue: VND 13,940 billion, up 10.55% compared to 2024 (VND 12,610 billion).
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): VND 745.7 billion, up 10.96% compared to 2024 (VND 672 billion).
- Net Profit After Tax (NPAT): VND 599.7 billion, up 11.43% compared to 2024 (VND 538.2 billion).
- ROA: 7.84% — up 0.21% compared to 7.64% in 2024. The Corporation is increasingly utilizing its assets more efficiently to generate profit.
- ROE: 30.50% — a significant increase of 2.63% compared to 27.87% in 2024. This is a very positive ROE level, reflecting high efficiency in the use of shareholders’ equity.

The Corporation has successfully achieved its business targets, ensuring growth compared to the same period and the planned objectives. All three key performance indicators showed consistent growth in 2025. Notably, the growth rate of net profit after tax (11.43%) is higher than the revenue growth rate (10.55%), indicating that the Corporation is improving cost control efficiency and enhancing profit margins.

### FINANCIAL STRUCTURE & LIQUIDITY

- Debt-to-Total Assets ratio: 0.75 (slightly up from 0.74) — Debt accounts for 75% of total assets, a common level in the industry; however, it requires continued monitoring to manage financial risk.
- Hệ số VCSH / Tổng tài sản: 0.25 (giảm nhẹ từ 0.26) — Vốn chủ sở hữu chiếm 25% tổng tài sản, phản ánh đòn bẩy tài chính ở mức cao.
- Equity-to-Total Assets ratio: 0.25 (slightly down from 0.26) — Equity accounts for 25% of total assets, reflecting a relatively high level of financial leverage.
- Debt-to-Equity ratio: 2.96 (up from 2.81) — Financial leverage has increased, indicating greater use of debt financing relative to equity, though still within a generally safe level (below 3x).
- Current ratio: 1.18 (up from 1.17) — Current assets are sufficient to cover short-term liabilities, ensuring a safe level of liquidity.
- Quick ratio: 1.11 (up significantly from 1.03) — A notable improvement, indicating stronger immediate liquidity without reliance on inventory.

The financial structure remains debt-oriented; however, both short-term and quick liquidity ratios have improved, reflecting better cash flow management by the Corporation in 2025.

### OPERATING EFFICIENCY — ASSET TURNOVER

- Inventory turnover: 25.03 times in 2025 vs. 16.75 times in 2024, an increase of 8.28 times (+49.46%) — a remarkable improvement, indicating faster inventory turnover, reduced storage costs, and lower risk of stock accumulation.
- Accounts receivable turnover: 7.42 times in 2025 vs. 6.35 times in 2024, an increase of 1.07 times (+16.89%) — the Corporation has improved its debt collection efficiency, enhancing cash flow and reducing credit risk.

Both operating efficiency indicators improved significantly in 2025. Inventory turnover increased by nearly 50%, reflecting highly effective short-term asset management. The increase in receivables turnover demonstrates stronger debt recovery performance, supported by close direction from the Executive Board and coordination among relevant departments.

### SUMMARY AND ASSESSMENT

- **Strong growth**: Revenue increased by 10.55%, while net profit after tax rose by 11.43%. ROE reached 30.50%, reflecting a very high return on equity.
- **Financial stability**: Both short-term and quick liquidity ratios improved. Equity increased by 10.84%, driven by internal accumulation.
- **Operational efficiency**: Inventory turnover increased by 49.46%, and receivables turnover increased by 16.89%, reflecting excellent short-term asset management.
- **Shareholder value creation**: Dividend payment reached VND 245.9 billion in 2025 (~45.5% of distributed profit), while VND 159.5 billion was allocated to reinvestment for development.
- **In 2025, the Corporation achieved comprehensive results, with revenue and profit growth, improved operational efficiency, strengthened financial capacity, and a stable dividend policy that remains attractive to shareholders.**

### PRESERVATION AND GROWTH OF EQUITY

- Equity: VND 2,067 billion, an increase of VND 202 billion (+10.84%) compared to the beginning of the year (VND 1,865 billion).
- Contributed capital: VND 1,144 billion, unchanged.
- Retained earnings after tax (undistributed): VND 654 billion, an increase of VND 53.5 billion (+8.90%) compared to the beginning of the year (VND 600.8 billion).

Equity growth is entirely driven by retained earnings, while contributed capital remains unchanged. This demonstrates the Corporation’s ability to accumulate internal resources without requiring additional capital contributions from shareholders. Accordingly, the Corporation has effectively preserved and increased its equity in line with principles of sustainable financial management.

### CASH FLOW ANALYSIS

- Net cash flow from operating activities: VND 1,455.4 billion, an increase of VND 314.9 billion (+27.6%) compared to VND 1,140.5 billion in 2024.
- Net cash flow for the year: VND 818.6 billion, a strong increase of VND 751.8 billion (+1,125%) compared to VND 66.8 billion in 2024.

Cash flow from operating activities increased significantly by 27.6%, reflecting high-quality earnings. Notably, net cash flow for the year increased more than elevenfold, demonstrating the Corporation’s strong cash accumulation capability and highly effective debt recovery in 2025.

### PROFIT DISTRIBUTION IN 2025

Total profit distribution: VND 540.82 billion.

- Dividend payment: 45.47%
- Development Investment Fund: 29.49%
- Bonus and Welfare Fund: 25.04%

Dividends account for the largest proportion (45.5%), followed by the Development Investment Fund (29.5%) and the Bonus & Welfare Fund (25.0%). The Corporation has achieved a balanced allocation between rewarding shareholders and reinvesting in future development plans.
