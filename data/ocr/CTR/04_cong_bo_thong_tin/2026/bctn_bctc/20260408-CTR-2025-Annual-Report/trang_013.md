# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## DEVELOPMENT STAGES

### 1995 – 2003
- Establishment of the Survey and Design Enterprise and the Construction Enterprise (the predecessors of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation) under the Military Electronics and Telecommunications Company.
- The enterprise carried out foundation construction and installation of antenna towers domestically and internationally with high quality standards such as: the 120-meter antenna tower for Tuyen Quang Television Station, the 140-meter antenna tower for Lao National Television Station, and antenna towers for Lao Telecom in Vientiane.

### 2004 – 2009
- The Construction Enterprise was renamed Viettel Construction Company.
- In 2006, the company officially changed its operating model from a dependent accounting unit to an independent accounting entity under the new name: Viettel Construction One-Member State-Owned Limited Liability Company.
- In December 2007, the company began implementing and deploying network construction projects in the Kingdom of Cambodia.
- Received a Certificate of Merit from the Ministry of Defence.
- Received the Certificate of Merit from the Ministry of Defence and the Prime Minister of Vietnam, and the Second-Class Fatherland Defense Order.

### 2010 – 2017
- In 2010, Viettel Construction focused strongly on developing 3G network. Viettel Group’s 2G and 3G networks infrastructure became the largest in Vietnam.
- On January 15, 2010, the Minister of Defence made a decision on transforming Viettel Construction One-Member State-Owned Limited Liability Company into Viettel Construction Joint Stock Company.
- In 2011, Viettel Construction organized deployment forces to carry out construction projects in Haiti.
- In 2014, the company established three overseas subsidiaries in Cameroon, Burundi and Tanzania.
- In 2015, the company completed procedures with the Ministry of Planning and Investment to obtain operating licenses in two markets: Laos and Myanmar.

### 2018 – 2020
- Became a leading digital-based technical service provider in Vietnam.
- Officially operated under 5 core business pillars: Infrastructure Leasing Investment, Civil Construction, Information Technology, Integrated Solutions, and Operations & Maintenance.
- In 2020, revenue reached VND 6,381 billion (106% of plan) and profit reached VND 274 billion (136% of plan).
- Achieved the First-Class Fatherland Defense Order, the Gold Award in the International Business Awards 2020, the People’s Choice Stevie Awards 2020 and VNR500.

### 2021 – 2025
- Became an investor, operator, and provider of high-efficiency smart infrastructure and construction services.
- In 2023, Viettel Construction recorded revenue of VND 11,399.4 billion, maintaining its position as Vietnam’s No.1 TowerCo.
- In 2025, revenue reached nearly VND 14,057 billion with net profit of VND 600 billion.
- Awarded the title of Hero of Labour.
