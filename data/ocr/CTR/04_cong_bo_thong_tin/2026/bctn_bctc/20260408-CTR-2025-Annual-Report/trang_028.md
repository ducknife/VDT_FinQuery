# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## 2. NUMBER OF EMPLOYEES

### Average number of employees in 2025: 10,169 people

### Employee-related policies

At VCC, human capital is recognized as one of the most critical assets of the Corporation. VCC ensures that nearly 11,000 employees are provided with comprehensive policies and benefits, including:

- The Corporation fully complies with all statutory regulations and ensures employees are entitled to all mandatory benefits, including social insurance, annual leave, statutory leave, health insurance participation, periodic health check-ups and corporate cultural activities.

- In addition to salary, bonuses and statutory welfare regimes (social, health, and unemployment insurance), the Corporation has developed differentiated welfare programs aimed at comprehensive care for employees and their families. Annually, all official employees undergo periodic health check-ups at reputable hospitals, fully funded by the Corporation. Family members of officers, professional soldiers, and defense employees are also provided with full health insurance coverage. For probationary employees who are not eligible for mandatory social insurance yet, the Corporation provides 24/7 accident insurance coverage, ensuring continuous protection and support.

- On an annual basis, during major holidays and festive occasions, VCC provides both cash and in-kind gifts to employees, including on April 30 & May 1 (Reunification Day & International Workers’ Day), September 2 (National Day), December 22 & New Year, Lunar New Year (Tet), Viettel Group’s Anniversary (June 1), and VCC’s Anniversary (October 30), among others.

- Employees are entitled to an annual 5-day vacation program, with expenses fully covered by the Corporation at VND 6 million per person.

- VCC fosters a safe working environment, promotes a proactive learning culture, and provides career advancement opportunities for all employees.

- The Corporation continues to develop high-quality human resources in alignment with local communities, which is one of the key pillars of its sustainable development strategy.

- VCC identifies Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) as a core element of its sustainable development strategy, aiming to protect employee rights, ensure operational stability, and support long-term growth. OSH practices are implemented in full compliance with Vietnamese legal regulations while progressively aligning with advanced international standards and best practices.

- The Corporation recognizes that the value created by each individual contributes to the overall success of the organization. Without discrimination based on gender, age, or ethnic background, including vulnerable groups in society, Viettel Construction is committed to building a dynamic, transparent, and fair working environment.

- The Corporation embraces the concept of a “learning organization” with the principle of “proactive learning,” whereby each individual defines their own learning pathway to enhance performance and effectively fulfill their roles.
