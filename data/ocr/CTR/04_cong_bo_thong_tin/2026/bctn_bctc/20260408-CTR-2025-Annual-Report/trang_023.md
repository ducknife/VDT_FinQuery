# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## TOOLS

The year 2025 marked a significant transformation for VCC in the digitalization of Risk Management, with the application of data analytics, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Machine Learning into both governance activities and daily operations across the Corporation. One of the key achievements was the implementation of a Business Intelligence (BI) reporting system, enabling the conversion of risk management data into visual dashboards and charts. This supports the Executive Board in monitoring the effectiveness of inspection and supervision activities against the approved plans, including the number of processes, control points (checkpoints) and inspection frequency at 34 Construction Branches nationwide.

VCC has also developed and continuously updated a risk library by business area, integrated early warning tools based on data analytics and established a repository of common errors. These tools are embedded into the software system to support employees in quick reference, timely risk identification, and improved work quality. As a result, they contribute to standardizing risk management practices, enhancing proactive risk prevention and improving overall operational efficiency across the Corporation.

## GOVERNANCE

Internal control activities at VCC are implemented under a risk-based approach, focusing resources on high-risk areas and key themes. In 2025, the internal control system conducted monitoring and supervision across 38 key topics with a focus on project implementation progress, asset management and utilization efficiency, as well as cash flow and receivables control at construction sites. Control activities have shifted from standalone inspections to cross-functional inspection teams, enabling the identification of systemic issues, root cause analysis, and the development of comprehensive corrective solutions, thereby minimizing business risks in a thorough and sustainable manner.

Inspection topics are carefully selected with clear priorities, focusing on key risks that directly impact the Corporation’s business objectives. Accordingly, internal control activities go beyond error detection, playing an advisory role in supporting the Executive Board and business units to enhance governance and operational efficiency.

VCC has also established a reward and penalty mechanism linked to risk management and compliance responsibilities. Individuals and teams with positive contributions to risk management and effective, practical solutions are promptly recognized and rewarded. At the same time, the Corporation strictly enforces regulations on handling violations, assigning individual accountability, and requiring compensation for damages where applicable, thereby strengthening discipline, accountability and a culture of compliance across the organization.
