# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## 4. Activities of Independent Board Members

The independent member of the Board of Directors, Mr. Nguyen Quang Khai, attended 10 out of 11 Board meetings and provided important opinions on corporate governance, sustainable development and the technology system of the Corporation.

### Assessment on the Company’s operations by the Independent Member of the Board of Directors

- In the final year of implementing the 2021–2025 strategy of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation, the Executive Board has decisively, closely and promptly made necessary adjustments to the organizational structure and business orientation to capture opportunities from breakthrough changes across the political system, while adapting to market fluctuations and adverse impacts from climate and natural disasters. As a result, the Corporation successfully fulfilled its 2025 production and business targets, made significant contributions to the Group’s 5G network infrastructure development, and participated in major national infrastructure projects.

- The Board of Directors demonstrated a strong strategic leadership role by thoroughly preparing for the 2026–2030 period and implementing large-scale organizational restructuring, thereby laying a solid foundation for long-term growth. Supervisory activities were carried out comprehensively, from the strategic level to on-site operations, contributing to improved operational efficiency and project quality. The adoption of G20/OECD governance standards and the review and enhancement of internal regulations reflect a strong commitment to improving corporate governance practices. Transactions with related parties were controlled in compliance with regulations, ensuring transparency and remaining within the scope approved by the General Meeting of Shareholders. However, continued close monitoring is required to solve weaknesses identified during inspections and supervision in order to strengthen sustainability in governance and compliance.

The Board of Directors and the Corporation have identified corporate governance as a key factor in enhancing competitiveness and serving as a driving force for business development. Accordingly, the Corporation has defined a roadmap to improve the effectiveness of its corporate governance activities, including:

- Establishing supporting committees under the Board of Directors
- Issuing a set of evaluation and scoring criteria for the Board of Directors, its members, and members of the Executive Board
- Developing and disclosing the annual and term-based work plan of the Board of Directors
