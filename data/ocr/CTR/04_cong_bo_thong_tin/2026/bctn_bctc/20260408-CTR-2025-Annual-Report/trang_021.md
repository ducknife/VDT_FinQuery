# ANNUAL REPORT 2025 - VIETTEL CONSTRUCTION

## HUMAN RESOURCE RISK

With project and construction activities deployed nationwide, human resource risk is one of the key challenges for enterprises operating in the construction and operations & exploitation sectors. The Corporation identifies people as both a core asset and a critical risk factor in the absence of sufficient personnel with adequate competence, experience and succession capability to ensure operational continuity and strategic execution.

To proactively manage risks related to workforce fluctuations and shortages of key personnel, the Corporation has implemented synchronized programs such as “Employee Experience Excellence” and “Talent Management”, aimed at enhancing engagement, retention and the development of high-quality human resources. In parallel, the Corporation has developed and operated a Performance Management System (PMS), linking compensation to productivity and actual employee capabilities, thereby creating motivation, improving labor efficiency and optimizing resource utilization.

The Corporation also focuses on building a professional, transparent working environment that encourages innovation and personal development. A strong learning culture is promoted through continuous training programs that enhance professional expertise, management skills, and adaptability for employees at all levels. In addition, successor team planning and development is systematically implemented based on a clearly defined framework and structured career progression paths. This approach creates development opportunities for younger employees while ensuring a sustainable talent pool capable of meeting the Group’s long-term strategic objectives. Key Risk Indicators (KRIs) related to fluctuations in key personnel are regularly monitored, enabling the Executive Board to timely identify risk trends and take appropriate response measures, thereby ensuring the stability and sustainable development of the Corporation.

## PROJECT SCHEDULE AND QUALITY RISK

Project schedule and quality risks are critical factors that directly impact the Corporation’s brand reputation and business performance, particularly for infrastructure projects such as BTS stations, solar energy systems, as well as B2C and B2B civil construction works. Potential risks, including site clearance legal issues, supply chain disruptions, manpower shortages or force majeure events at construction sites may cause delays in project execution and affect the fulfillment of contractual commitments to customers.

To proactively coping with challenges, the Corporation has developed and implemented an Integrated Operation Center (IOC) construction management software, transitioning from manual management methods to a fully digital, real-time monitoring system. Accordingly, the Corporation aims for 100% of projects to be monitored in terms of progress and quality via mobile applications, with continuous updates of visual data, site diaries, and key control milestones. This enables the Executive Board to promptly capture project status, detect risks early and make timely adjustments when necessary.

In parallel, the Corporation has developed and implemented a standardized evaluation framework to quantify, assess and periodically rank partners, contractors and suppliers. The development of a sustainable supply chain, along with the selection of capable and reputable partners who can meet schedule and quality requirements is a key solution to minimize operational risks, enhance project execution efficiency, and ensure the Corporation’s stable and sustainable long-term development.
