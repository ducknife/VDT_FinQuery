HEREBY RESOLVES:

Article 1. Approval of the 2025 dividend payment in cash and shares, with July 10, 2026 as the record date:

2.1. Cash dividends
- Dividend rate: 15% per share (each share entitles the holder to VND 1,500).
- Payment date: July 10, 2026.
- Payment method: Bank transfer.
- Implementation place:
  + For deposited securities: Shareholders will implement procedures to receive dividend at the securities company where shareholders opened securities depository account.
  + For undeposit securities:
    ❖ For Shareholders who have registered a dividend receiving account: The Corporation will make the bank transfer to the shareholders on July 30, 2026.
    ❖ For Shareholders who have not registered an account or wish to change their dividend receiving account: Shareholders are requested to contact the Shareholder Relations department – the Board of Directors' Office of the Corporation to complete the necessary procedures.
