**(1)** The construction contract for the structural works and exterior finishing of 107 low-rise residential units (excluding piling works) under the Trung Minh A New Urban Area Project was signed between Central Park Trading Company Limited and the Corporation. **(2)** The construction contract for technical infrastructure works (including site leveling, roads, the National Highway 6 intersection, lake embankments, subdivision retaining walls, project boundary retaining walls, stormwater and wastewater drainage systems) under the Trung Minh B New Urban Area Project was signed between the Corporation and Central Park Trading Company Limited. The project is located in Hoa Binh Ward, Phu Tho Province. The contract period is 12 months from the effective date of the contract and upon handover of the construction site. As at 31 December 2025, 13.9% of the contract value had been accepted by the investor.

As at 31 December 2025, 6.01% of the contract value had been accepted by the investor.

- The construction contract for piling works, foundations, structural works and exterior finishing of 184 low-rise residential units – Zone 1 and Zone 2 (excluding doors, water tanks and septic tanks), signed between Eco Pearl City Group Joint Stock Company and the Corporation. - The construction contract for site leveling, roads, stormwater drainage and wastewater drainage works, signed between Eco Pearl City Group Joint Stock Company and the Corporation.
