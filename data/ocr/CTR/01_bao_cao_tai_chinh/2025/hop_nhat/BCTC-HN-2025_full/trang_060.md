## Year 2025

### 1. GENERAL INFORMATION

#### Forms of Ownership
The Corporation was converted from the state-owned enterprise Viettel Construction One Member Limited Liability Company into a Joint Stock Company under Decision No. 135/QD-BQP dated 15 January 2010 approving the geuitization plan and conversion of Viettel Construction Company under the Military Telecommunications Corporation (now the Military Industry and Telecommunications Group) into a joint stock company. The Corporation changed its name from Viettel Construction Joint Stock Company to Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation under the Resolution of the Shareholders' Meeting dated 19 June 2018. The Corporation operates under Business Registration Certificate No. 0104753865 issued by the Hanoi Authority for Planning and Investment (now known as the Hanoi Department of Finance) on 09 June 2010 and amended for the 19th time on 31 July 2025.

The Corporation's head office is located at No. 01 Giang Van Minh, Giang Vo Ward, Hanoi.

The Corporation's transaction office is located at No. 06 Pham Van Bach, Cau Giay ward, Hanoi.

The Corporation's charter capital is VND 1,143,858,790,000 equivalent to 114,385,879 shares, with the par value of VND 10,000 per share.

The number of employees of the Corporation and its subsidiaries as at 31 December 2025 was 10,385 people and 2,190 people, respectively (as at 01 January 2025 was 10,459 people and 1,981 people,

#### Business field
The Corporation operates in the fields of construction, operation of telecommunications networks, infrastructure leasing and the trade of commercial goods.

#### Business activities
- Construction of all types of houses, railways, roads, public works and other civil engineering works;
- Installation, repair, maintenance and servicing of telecommunications infrastructure;
- Installation, maintenance, repair and trading of electrical, electronic, telecommunications and information technology equipment;
- Leasing of infrastructure (BTS, DAS and undergrounding); solar power activities;
- Wired and wireless telecommunications;
- Construction, maintenance and operation of networks.

#### The Corporation’s operation in the year that affects the Consolidated Financial Statements
In 2025, the Corporation reorganized the organizational structure of its head office and implemented a restructuring of the Viettel Construction branch network across provinces and cities. Accordingly, the 63 provincial and municipal Viettel Construction branches were reviewed and reorganized into 34 branches. At the same time, operations of 29 branches were terminated in order to streamline the organizational structure, enhance management efficiency, and optimize production and business activities.
