# NOTES TO THE SEPARATE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
## Year 2025

### 1. GENERAL INFORMATION

#### Forms of Ownership
The Company was converted from a state-owned enterprise Viettel Construction Company Limited into a Joint Stock Company under Decision No. 135/QD-BQP dated 15 January 2010 on approving the plan and converting Viettel Construction Company under the Military Telecommunications Corporation (now known as the Military Industry and Telecommunications Group) into a Joint Stock Company. The Company changed its name from Viettel Construction Joint Stock Company to Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation under the Resolution of the Shareholders' Meeting dated 19 June 2018, operating under Business Registration Certificate No. 0104753865 issued by the Hanoi Authority for Planning and Investment (now known as the Hanoi Department of Finance) on 09 June 2010 and amended for the 19th time on 31 July 2025.

The Company's head office is located at No. 01 Giang Van Minh, Giang Vo ward, Hanoi.

The Company's transaction office is located at No. 06 Pham Van Bach, Cau Giay ward, Hanoi.

The Company's charter capital is VND 1,143,858,790,000 equivalent to 114,385,879 shares, with the par value of VND 10,000 per share.

The number of employees of the Corporation as at 31 December 2025 is 10,385 employees (as at 01 January 2025 was 10,459 employees).

#### Business field
The Corporation operates in the fields of construction, telecommunications network operation, infrastructure leasing and commercial goods trading.

#### Business activities
- Construction of all types of houses, railways, roads, public works and other civil engineering works;
- Installation, repair, maintenance and servicing of telecommunications infrastructure;
- Installation, maintenance, repair and trading of electrical, electronic, telecommunications, information technology and communication equipment;
- Infrastructure for rent business: BTS, DAS, Underground stations, Solar power business;
- Wired telecommunications, wireless telecommunications;
- Business of construction, maintenance and operation of network.

#### The Company's operation in the year that affects the Separate Financial Statements
In 2025, the Corporation reorganized the organizational structure of its head office divisions and implemented a restructuring of the Viettel Construction Branch network across provinces and cities. Accordingly, the 63 provincial and municipal Viettel Construction Branches were reviewed and reorganized into 34 branches. At the same time, operations of 29 branches were terminated in order to streamline the organizational structure, enhance management efficiency, and optimize production and business activities.
