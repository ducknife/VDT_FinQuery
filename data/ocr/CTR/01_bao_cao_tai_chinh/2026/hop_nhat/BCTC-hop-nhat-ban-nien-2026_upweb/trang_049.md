# Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation

## REPORT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGEMENT

The Board of Management of Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation ("the Corporation") presents its report and the Company's Interim Consolidated Financial Statements for the period from 01 January 2026 to 30 June 2026.

### THE CORPORATION

The Corporation was converted from the state-owned enterprise Viettel Construction One Member Limited Liability Company into a Joint Stock Company under Decision No. 135/QD-BQP dated 15 January 2010 approving the equitization plan and conversion of Viettel Construction Company under the Military Telecommunications Corporation (now the Military Industry and Telecommunications Group) into a joint stock company. The Corporation changed its name from Viettel Construction Joint Stock Company to Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation under the Resolution of the Shareholders' Meeting dated 19 June 2018. The Corporation operates under Business Registration Certificate No. 0104753865 issued by the Hanoi Authority for Planning and Investment (now known as the Hanoi Department of Finance) on 09 June 2010 and amended for the 20th time on 06 August 2026.

The Corporation's head office is located at No. 01 Giang Van Minh, Giang Vo ward, Hanoi.

The Corporation's transaction office is located at No. 06 Pham Van Bach, Cau Giay ward, Hanoi.
