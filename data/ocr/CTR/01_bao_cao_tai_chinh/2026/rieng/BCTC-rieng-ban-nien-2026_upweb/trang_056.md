# NOTES TO THE INTERIM SEPARATE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS  
*For the period from 01/01/2026 to 30/06/2026*

## 1. GENERAL INFORMATION

### 1.1. Forms of ownership

The Corporation was converted from the state-owned enterprise Viettel Construction One Member Limited Liability Company into a Joint Stock Company under Decision No. 135/QD-BQP dated 15 January 2010 approving the equitization plan and conversion of Viettel Construction Company under the Military Telecommunications Corporation (now the Military Industry and Telecommunications Group) into a joint stock company. The Corporation changed its name from Viettel Construction Joint Stock Company to Viettel Construction Joint Stock Corporation under the Resolution of the Shareholders' Meeting dated 19 June 2018. The Corporation operates under Business Registration Certificate No. 0104753865 issued by the Hanoi Authority for Planning and Investment (now known as the Hanoi Department of Finance) on 09 June 2010 and amended for the 20th time on 06 August 2026.

The Corporation’s head office is located at No. 01 Giang Van Minh, Giang Vo ward, Hanoi.

The Corporation’s transaction office is located at No. 06 Pham Van Bach, Cau Giay ward, Hanoi.

The Corporation’s charter capital is VND 1,143,858,790,000 equivalent to 114,385,879 shares, with the par value of VND 10,000 per share.

The number of employees of the Corporation as at 30 June 2026 is 10,033 employees (as at 01 January 2026 was 10,385 employees).

### 1.2. Business field

The Corporation operates in the fields of construction, operation of telecommunications networks, infrastructure leasing and the trade of commercial goods.

### 1.3. Business activities

- Construction of all types of houses, railways, roads, public works and other civil engineering works;
- Installation, repair, maintenance and servicing of telecommunications infrastructure;
- Installation, maintenance, repair and trading of electrical, electronic, telecommunications, information technology and communication equipment;
- Infrastructure for rent business: BTS, DAS, Underground stations, Solar power energy business;
- Wired telecommunications, wireless telecommunications;
- Construction, maintenance and operation of networks.
