Trong 6 tháng đầu năm 2026, ghi nhận doanh thu bán hàng và cung cấp dịch vụ đạt 7.146,67 tỷ đồng, tăng 2053,20 tỷ tương ứng 40,31% so với cùng kỳ 2025, giá vốn hàng bán đạt 6.671,16 tỷ, tăng 1.967,64 tỷ tương ứng 41,83%, doanh thu tài chính đạt 94,25 tỷ, tăng 53,75 tỷ tương ứng 132,74%, chi phí tài chính đạt 77,84 tỷ, tăng 34,65 tỷ tương ứng 80,22%, chi phí quản lý doanh nghiệp đạt 119,42 tỷ, tăng 42,61 tỷ tương ứng 55,47% so với cùng kỳ 2025. In the first 6 months of 2026, revenue from sales of goods rendering of service reached 7,146.67 billions VND, which rose by 2,053.20 billions VND, representing a 40.31% growth. Cost of goods sold reached 6,671.16 billion VND, which rose by 1,967.64 billions, representing a 41.83% growth. Financial income reached 94.25 billions VND, which rose by 53.75 billions VND, representing a 132.74% growth. Financial expense reached 77.84 billions, which rose 34.65 billions, representing a 80.22% growth. General and administrative expense reached 119.42 billions, which rose by 42.61 billion VND, equivalent to 55.47% compared to the same period in 2025. Accordingly, profit after tax in the separate financial statements increased by 20.76% over the same period in 2025.

- Profit after tax for the first 6 months of 2026 recognized on the consolidated financial statements reached 321.81 billions VND, rising by 54.88 billions that equivalent to a 20.56% growth compared to the same period in 2025:

In the first 6 months of 2026, revenue from sales of goods rendering of service reached 7,958.84 billions, which rose 1,984.03 billions, representing a 33.21% growth.
