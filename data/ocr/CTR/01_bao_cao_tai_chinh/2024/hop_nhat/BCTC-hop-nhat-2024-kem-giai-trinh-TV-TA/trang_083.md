### 36. FINANCIAL INSTRUMENTS

#### Financial risk management
The Company's financial risks may include market risk, credit risk and liquidity risk. The Company has developed its control system to ensure the reasonable balance between cost of incurred risks and cost of risk management. The Board of Management of the Company is responsible for monitoring the risk management process to ensure the appropriate balance between risk and risk control.

#### Market risk
The Company will bear the market risks such as exchange rates, interest rates.

##### Exchange rate risk:
The Company bears the risk of exchange rate due to the fluctuation in fair value of future cash flows of a financial instrument in line with changes in exchange rates if revenues and expenses of the Company are denominated in foreign currencies other than VND.
